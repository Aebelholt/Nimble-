# Draft: the mushroom cellar

Status: proposal, revised with Seb's calls. Not canon until Seb gives the go.

The goal: something to work toward. Stats rise by growing mushrooms in the back room, once it's dug out and taken from the Broodmother. Each kind raises one stat.

## The chain

1. **The scratching.** Once the Hearth reaches Simple, the Broodmother scratches behind the chimney at night. She only hears it on nights she sleeps at home.
2. **The Shovel.** It's on the workbench list: 2 Wood, 1 Stone, with the Workbench at Simple. Agnes and Pil can tell her it's what a mouse needs for frozen earth. *(Still open: how she learns of it.)*
3. **Dig it out.** That's the back room's Simple rung: a Shovel and a Mend. It wakes the Broodmother.
4. **The Broodmother.** Her eggs, her warm earth, and your mother's last letter.
5. **The cellar.** The back room's Good rung, blank today: **mushroom beds, 2 Wood, 1 Fiber.**
6. **Spawn.** A piece of mushroom or mycelium, brought home. It takes a slot until it's seeded. She can carry or keep spawn before she has the cellar.
7. **Seed, wait, harvest, eat.**

| **Foxfire** | WIL | Tobben, at the Brook | Once she has helped Tobben and crossed on his stones both ways, he gives her the mycelium compound. The light he has is this |

## Growing

- **Seed** at the hearth, as the night's one thing. One bed per kind. The cellar holds three.
- **It fruits after three nights** with the hearth lit. A night without a fire doesn't count. Tick ☐☐☐ on the Burrow sheet.
- **Harvest and eat it** as a Cook. The first harvest of each kind raises that stat by **d3**, both max and current. Later harvests are 2 Food.

The most any mouse gains is 3d3 across her three stats, about +2 each. Sofie's mouse ends near STR 11, DEX 10 and WIL 10.

## The Broodmother is a wall, so give her doors

Odds from `tools/brood_sim.py` (full table in `tools/brood_results.md`), fought to a finish against 12hp, STR 12, Armour 1, d8 bite to DEX:

| Mouse | Stick | Spear | Spear, set up |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| HP 2 | 0% | 7% | 9% |
| HP 4 | 0% | 14% | 17% |

A straight fight is not the answer. Doors that already exist in the rules:

- **Murder.** A fox against a spider is a fair fight. A fox on red caps is more than fair.
- **The cold.** Spiders are slow in the cold. Dig through to the outside on a Bitter cold day and she acts first every round.
- **Fire.** Fire clears webs at once. It can strip the egg sacs from the walls, and with them her reason to stay.
- **Underoak.** With three dots, a neighbour might come and hold a light.
