# Draft: the mushroom cellar

Status: proposal. Nothing here is canon until Seb approves it.

The goal: something to work toward. Stats rise by growing mushrooms in the back room, once it's dug out and taken from the Broodmother. Each kind of mushroom raises one stat.

## The chain

1. **The scratching.** When the Hearth reaches Simple, something scratches behind the wall at night. That's existing canon.
2. **Murder digs at the chimney.** From outside, Murder scratches at the earth behind the chimney stones, nose down. A fox knows warm ground. That's how she learns there's a room back there worth digging.
3. **The Shovel.** It's already on the workbench list: 2 Wood, 1 Stone, with the Workbench at Simple. Agnes and Pil can say what a mouse needs to dig through frozen earth if she asks.
4. **Dig it out.** That's the back room's Simple rung: a Shovel and a Mend. It wakes the Broodmother.
5. **The Broodmother.** Her eggs, her warm earth, and your mother's last letter.
6. **The cellar.** The back room's Good rung, which is blank today: **mushroom beds, 2 Wood, 1 Fiber.**
7. **Spawn.** A piece of a mushroom with its rotten wood, brought home. It takes a slot.
8. **Seed, wait, harvest, eat.**

## The Broodmother is a wall, so give her doors

Odds from `tools/brood_sim.py` (full table in `tools/brood_results.md`), fought to a finish against 12hp, STR 12, Armour 1, d8 bite to DEX:

| HP 2 | 0% | 7% | 9% |
| HP 4 | 0% | 14% | 17% |

A straight fight is not the answer. Doors that already exist in the rules:

- **Murder.** A fox against a spider is a fair fight, and killing is what it's good at. Bringing Murder into the burrow is the obvious, earned answer.
- **The cold.** Spiders are slow in the cold. Dig through to the outside on a Bitter cold day, let the winter in, and she acts first every round.
- **Fire.** Fire clears webs at once and wakes everything nearby. It can strip her egg sacs from the walls, and with them her reason to stay.
- **Underoak.** With three dots, a neighbour might come and hold a light.

## Growing

- **Seed** at the hearth, as the night's one thing. One bed per kind. The cellar holds three.
- **It fruits after three nights** with the hearth lit. A night without a fire doesn't count. Tick ☐☐☐ on the Burrow sheet.
- **Harvest and eat it** as a Cook. The first harvest of each kind raises that stat by **d3**, both max and current. Later harvests are 2 Food.

So the most any mouse gains is 3d3 across her three stats, average +2 each. For Sofie's mouse that's STR 9 to 11, DEX 8 to 10 and WIL 8 to 10. You'd feel it, and it doesn't break anything.

## Murder

This fills one of the open slots in the log. **Gives:** knows where foxfire glows, and dug at the chimney first. Want and grief stay with you.
