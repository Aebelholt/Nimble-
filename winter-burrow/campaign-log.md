# Winter Burrow, campaign log

What happened at the table. Where play changed canon, `winter-burrow.md` and the GM sheet follow.

## Session 1

- The first encounter was a web-weaver, through the broken door. She fought until her DEX was gone.
- Rask found her the next morning. She spent the night at Underoak and met Nibs.
- On the way home she rolled the fox. She befriended it and gave it a hat. She told it it needed a name, and it chose one for what it's good at: **Murder**.
- She got home and fixed the door. **Door and tunnel: Simple.**
