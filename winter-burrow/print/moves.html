Roll only when the outcome is uncertain and failing costs something. Say what you're doing, and the GM names the move and the stat. Roll d20 against it.

d20 rollResult
Equal to or underDone
Up to 5 overAt a cost, and the GM picks it
More than 5 overFails

A cost is one you can see: an extra hour, a Warmth step, a slot left behind, a noise, d4 damage.

Advantage when a tool, a neighbour or a good plan helps. Disadvantage when a condition says so, or you're encumbered.

Pass or fail when a rule names the failure: save or wet, save or stuck.
