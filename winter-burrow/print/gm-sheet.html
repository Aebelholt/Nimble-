Winter Burrow

GM sheet · procedure

The hour loop

  1. Morning. Roll 2d6 for weather. She leaves home at her top die.
  2. Each hour, she marks it. Outdoors, she rolls Warmth, twice an hour at Night or in a snowstorm, never more. On 1–2 it steps down. At d4, a step down is a Chilled instead. Wet counts as a 1.
  3. Shelter: no roll, and the crow's ladder resets.
  4. Read the same die. By day, the crow. At Night, the owl.
  5. Arriving outdoors, she throws that hour's Warmth die with two d6 of different colours. First d6: 1 is an encounter, 2 an omen (tracks, a feather, a silence where there was birdsong). Second d6: the table.
  6. Crossing the Brook: DEX save or wet. Tobben's stones: no save.
  7. After a fight, roll Warmth.
  8. Home. Light the fire, one Hearth action, sleep. No fire, she sleeps Chilled. Door Forlorn: d6 each night, on a 1 something gets in.

Running it

  • Say "roll Warmth". Never give the odds. The die on the table is the whole message.
  • A save up to 5 over is a cost or a half success. Make the cost one she can see: an hour, a Warmth step, a dropped slot, a crow rung, a noise. A save with a named failure (save or wet, save or taken) stays pass or fail.
  • The hours are on the track where she can see them. Turning back is her call.
  • Don't announce what a room will turn up. Let her find it.
  • Foes take damage like she does: HP, then STR and a STR save. Fail and it flees or dies. Running from something faster (fox, jay, owl) is a DEX save, or it gets one more attack.
  • She can't die. When she goes down, end the scene and move to the next Morning. She wakes somewhere worse (where she fell, under snow, dragged off, or by a neighbour's fire once Underoak is full) with 1 HP, Warmth d4 and Injured. Clear all but one Chilled. Her bundles are lost. STR 0: she wakes at STR 1, Frostbitten too.
  • The Weir is far on purpose. It probably takes clothes, a tool, and a friend's tunnel. One evening may not get there, and that's fine.

Sky and ground

Each threat has one answer. Fight spiders, hide from the crow, avoid the owl, wait out the ants.

The crow, Morning to Dusk

No stat block, can't be fought. Every Warmth 1 rolled in the open moves it a rung. On open ground (Long Field, the Brook ice, the Larch clearing) a 2 counts too. Any Salvage or keepsake in the pack: each counting roll moves it two rungs. Wet isn't a rolled 1.

  1. A caw, far off.
  2. A black shape on a high branch. It turns its head when she moves.
  3. It drops. DEX save, or it takes a random pack slot and is gone. The ladder resets.

Resets under cover or at home. Show only the omens. Never name what it wants.

The owl, at Night

No ladder. In the open at Night, a Warmth 1 brings it (with a lit lantern, a 1 or 2). The roll still steps Warmth down. At most once an hour. It stoops: DEX save, no middle band.

Pass: talons close on snow. It can come again on the next 1. Fail: taken. She wakes at first light somewhere high, cold and alone (a branch, a beam, a ledge) with her pack, Warmth d4 and one Chilled. Getting down is the morning's first scene.

Fighting it is a last resort: 15hp, STR 15, DEX 15, d10 bite. Its critical damage is that it takes her.

Ant columns

A line across the path. Standing in it strips d3 Food a round. Going around costs an hour. The column moves on at Dusk.

Spiders

They live where it's warm and still: in shelter, down deep, and in the places worth reaching. A spider's place is out of the wind, so no Warmth roll there. That's the trap.

Winter Burrow

Opening

Start at Dusk with two hours of light left, snow just starting. She has nothing to burn.

The beech is where you left it, bigger than you remember. The door hangs off one hinge. Inside, the hearth is grey ash, and the cold has got into everything. On the wall is a calendar, the days crossed out in pencil until the middle of last month. Then nothing.

Ask: What did you bring from the city that you couldn't leave behind? It takes a slot, and it's hers to Remember.

Tonight's problem. Deadfall among the beech roots: an hour's work for 1 Wood. The Hedge is an hour away and has more. Without a fire she sleeps Chilled.

Marit

Marit kept the burrow alone after your parents went to the city. When word came that your mother had died, she stopped mending things. Late in autumn she walked to the Weir to fetch your mother's seed tin. The sisters had saved seed in it every year since they were girls, for the garden under the beech. The first snow caught her coming back. She is in the mill loft with a twisted ankle, rationing the seeds as food and ashamed of the state she left the burrow in. If she's found, she can be helped home, and a sled carries her. Nothing about how it ends is fixed.

The letter, in Aunt Marit's hand, late summer: Come home before the snow. There is something of your mother's I want you to have, and I'd rather give it to you here.

When she Remembers, give one small, concrete memory, never a conclusion. Her hands red from the cold, knitting by this fire, never a matching pair.

The wood

Hours from the beech, deep snow counted. Between places, the difference, at least 1. Every Brook crossing is a DEX save, or wet. A sled can't go into the Larch hollow, the Pit's lower cut or the Shed rafters.
