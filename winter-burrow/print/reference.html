Saves

StatCovers
STRForce, endurance, holding on
DEXSpeed, balance, dodging, moving quietly
WILNerve, noticing, persuading

Only roll when she's trying to avoid something bad, and say what failing will cost before she rolls. Equal or under: done. Up to 5 over: at a cost. More: fails. A save with a named failure (save or wet) is pass or fail.

Advantage when she's well prepared or equipped. Disadvantage when she's badly placed, badly equipped, or a condition says so.

Opposed. When she and someone else want the same thing, both save. The lowest roll that succeeds wins. If both fail, neither gets it.

Luck rolls

For anything that isn't her doing, like whether the cat is out or Nibs has honey today, give it a chance in 6 and roll d6. Equal or under, it happens. 1-in-6 is unlikely, 3-in-6 even, 5-in-6 likely.

Usage

Most things have 3 uses. At three, it's broken or spent.

ThingMarks a use
Weapons, bark shieldAfter a fight, on a d6 roll of 4–6
Stick, anything improvisedEvery fight
MatchesEach fire
LanternEach Night it's lit
Anything elseWhen used in a way that could break or spend it

Repair. A Make at the workbench clears every use on one thing, for 1 of what it's made from.

Fights, from the GM's side

  • A fight takes minutes and marks no hour. The Warmth roll after it is its cost.
  • A foe with no WIL listed has WIL 10.
  • A foe that's plainly outmatched makes a WIL save when the fight starts, or flees. A group makes one when half of it is down.
  • A foe that fails its STR save is beaten: it flees or dies. Critical damage in a stat block replaces that, and replaces her Injured-and-down.
  • Hampered (in a web, grabbed, through cover): d4. Set up (stuck, unaware, a trick that worked): d12.

Stats at 0

STR 0She goes down and wakes at STR 1, Frostbitten
DEX 0She can't move until a night at home brings a point back
WIL 0She's out of the scene, as if she'd gone down

She never dies. Going down: next Morning, somewhere worse, 1 HP, Warmth d4, Injured, all but one Chilled cleared, bundles lost.

Who talks

Mice and the neighbours talk. Birds, foxes, cats and spiders don't. When a creature's mood isn't obvious, roll 2d6.

2d6MoodAsk yourself
2HostileHow has she angered it?
3–5UnfriendlyHow could it be appeased?
6–8UnsureWhat would win it over?
9–11TalkativeWhat would it trade?
12HelpfulHow can it help?

Time and rest

  • A task is an hour: gathering, searching, cutting through a web, going around the ants.
  • A look around, a short talk or a fight is minutes and marks nothing.
  • HP comes back only with sleep. There are no short rests.
  • A night without sleep is Exhausted.

Not in this game

Magic, pips and prices, XP, levels and grit, hirelings and warbands, banks, hexes and watches, Mausritter's weather table.
