Winter Burrow

a mouse come home

You are a mouse come home from the city. Your parents are dead. The family burrow under the old beech has gone cold, and the aunt who kept it is gone. The snow is coming in.

Go out, bring things home, make the burrow warm again. Find out where she went.

Make a mouse

  1. Roll 3d6 for STR, DEX and WIL in order, keeping the two highest each time. Swap two if you like.
  2. Roll d6 for HP.
  3. Roll d6 for your city trade and take both items.
  4. Take a city coat (Warmth d8), a sharpened stick (d6), 2 Food and the letter.
  5. Name yourself.

The day

Mark an hour for every hour of travel and every task. Between two places, it's the difference in their hours from home, at least 1.

Core rules

Saves. When the outcome is uncertain and failing costs something, roll d20 against STR, DEX or WIL. With advantage, roll two and take the lower. With disadvantage, take the higher.

d20 rollResult
Equal to or underYou do it
Up to 5 overYou do it at a cost, or only partly. The GM says which
More than 5 overIt fails

When a rule names the failure (save or get wet, save or stuck), it's pass or fail.

Slots. You have 2 paw slots, 2 body slots and 6 pack slots. Every item, bundle and condition takes one. Food stacks 3 to a slot. Over the limit you are encumbered: disadvantage on everything and no running.

Conditions sit in a pack slot until cleared.

When a condition has no free pack slot, drop something in the snow to make room. When every pack slot holds a condition, you go down in the snow.

Harm. Damage comes off HP, then STR. After STR damage, make a STR save or take Injured and go down. Attacks always hit, so roll your weapon die. Almost everything out there is bigger than you. Running and hiding are always on the table. After a fight, roll Warmth, because the sweat freezes.

Weapons. A stick, a needle or anything improvised does d6. A wrench or a hatchet does d6, or d8 held in both paws. A spear does d10 and needs both paws.

Chassis: Mausritter. Anything not written here works as Mausritter says.

Warmth

Warmth is one die: d4, d6, d8, d10 or d12. Your clothes set your top die. You leave home at your top.

Roll Warmth every hour you mark outdoors, twice an hour at Night. On a 1 or 2, step it down one size. Getting wet counts as rolling a 1.

At d4, a step down puts a Chilled in a pack slot instead, and the die stays at d4.

Warming up

  • A hot drink steps it up one, never above your top. Brew one at the hearth for 1 Food. It takes a slot and stays hot until Dusk.
  • Shelter out of the wind means no roll that hour.
  • A fire puts you back at your top. Each hour beside it clears one Chilled, and a night beside it clears them all.
  • A fire away from home costs 1 Wood and needs somewhere dry.

The Burrow

The burrow is a sheet of rooms, on the back of your character sheet. Every room starts Forlorn, more an idea than a room. Pay a rung's cost on a Mend to climb it. A room's first climb turns something up.

Hearth

Come home, light the fire, then do one thing before you sleep. No fire, you sleep Chilled.

  • Cook. Spend 1 Food, your day's Food, for a full belly.
  • Mend. Restore a room.
  • Make. Make a tool at the workbench.
  • Knit. Knit a garment. A scarf can be knitted anywhere.
  • Remember. Hold something you brought home. The GM tells you what you remember.

Gathering and haul

Spend an hour at a place to gather one bundle of what's there: Wood, Stone, Fiber, Food or Salvage. A bundle takes a slot. Some things need a tool. Whatever you find out there takes a slot too, and you choose what goes home.

You eat 1 Food a day. A meal cooked at home gives you a full belly: your first Warmth roll the next day can't step down.
