# Winter Burrow

## You

You are a mouse come home from the city. Your parents are dead. The family burrow under the old beech has gone cold, and the aunt who kept it is gone. The snow is coming in.

Go out, bring things home, make the burrow warm again. Find out where she went.

Built on Mausritter. Everything you play by is in these pages. When something isn't, the GM makes a ruling and writes it down.

## Make a mouse

1. Roll 3d6 for STR, DEX and WIL in order, keeping the two highest each time. Swap two if you like.
2. Roll d6 for HP.
3. Roll d6 for your city trade and take both items.
4. Take a city coat (Warmth d8), a sharpened stick (d6), 2 Food and the letter.
5. Name yourself.

| d6 | City trade | Item | Item |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Seamstress | Needle (d6, light: wears out slower than a stick) | Thimble of buttons, good for trade |
| 2 | Lamplighter | Matches, 3 uses: a fire anywhere dry | Brass snuffer |
| 3 | Porter | Rope | Shoulder strap, +1 pack slot |
| 4 | Baker | Jar of honey, 3 Food | Copper pan: 1 Food cooks two full bellies, yours and a guest's |
| 5 | Clerk | Pencil and ledger: a route you've walked and written down costs 1 hour less, never below 1 | Spectacles |
| 6 | Factory hand | Wrench (d6/d8), opens human things | Tin cup: hot drinks step Warmth up twice |

## Core rules

**Saves.** When the outcome is uncertain and failing costs something, roll d20 against STR, DEX or WIL. Equal to or under: you do it. Up to 5 over: you do it at a cost, or only partly, and the GM says which. More than 5 over: it fails. With advantage, roll two and take the lower. With disadvantage, take the higher.

When a rule names the failure (save or get wet, save or stuck), it's pass or fail: the band doesn't apply.

**Slots.** You have 2 paw slots, 2 body slots and 6 pack slots. Every item, bundle and condition takes one. Food stacks 3 to a slot. Over the limit you are encumbered, which means disadvantage on everything and no running.

**Conditions.** A condition sits in a pack slot until it is cleared.

| Condition | You get it | Cleared by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chilled | Warmth would step down from d4 | An hour by a fire clears one. A night by a fire clears them all |
| Hungry | A day without Food | Eating |
| Exhausted | A night without sleep | A night in a nest |
| Injured | Hurt badly. Disadvantage on STR and DEX | Three nights at home |
| Frostbitten | The GM says so. Disadvantage on DEX | Three nights at home |
| Wet | Going through ice, sleet, a soaking. Warmth steps down on 1–3 | An hour by a fire |

When a condition has no free pack slot, drop something in the snow to make room. When every pack slot holds a condition, you go down in the snow.

**The day.** Mark an hour for every hour of travel and every task. Places are joined by paths, and each path has its hours.

Morning ☐☐☐☐ · Afternoon ☐☐☐☐ · Dusk ☐☐ · Night

No XP, no levels. The burrow is how you grow.

## Fighting

Almost everything out there is bigger than you. Running and hiding are always on the table.

**Who goes first.** If you strike before they know you're there, you go first. Otherwise, make a DEX save to act before them. Then keep that order.

**Your turn.** Move, and do one thing: attack, run, hide, talk, or try something risky like a trip or a feint. Then each of them acts.

**Attacks always hit.** Roll your weapon die, take off the target's Armour, and the rest comes off its HP.

- **Hampered** (stuck in a web, grabbed, striking through cover): roll d4, whatever the weapon.
- **Set up** (a trick that worked, a target stuck, asleep or caught unawares): roll d12.

| Weapon | Damage |
| --- | --- |
| Stick, needle, anything improvised | d6 |
| Wrench, hatchet | d6, or d8 in both paws |
| Spear | d10, both paws |
| A weapon in each paw | Roll both, keep the best |

**Taking damage.** Damage comes off HP first, then STR. After any STR damage, make a STR save. Pass and you keep going. Fail and you take Injured and go down. A bite "to DEX" works the same way, but past HP it comes off DEX and the save is DEX. At DEX 0 you can't move.

The same holds for them. When one fails its save, it's beaten: it flees or dies. Some list their own critical damage, which replaces this.

**Running.** On your turn, run. From anything slower, or busy, you're away. From anything faster, make a DEX save: pass and you're away, fail and it gets one more attack before you are. Encumbered, you can't run.

**Hiding.** Get into cover. If it's looking for you, make a DEX save.

**After a fight**, roll Warmth, because the sweat freezes. Roll d6 for each weapon you used: on 4–6, mark a use. A stick or anything improvised marks a use every fight. Three uses and it breaks. A new sharpened stick takes an hour at home and 1 Wood.

**Healing.** HP comes back with sleep (see the Nest). Lost STR, DEX and WIL come back one point each per night at home.

**Going down.** You don't die in Winter Burrow. Going down ends the scene, and the GM moves time on. You wake at the next Morning somewhere worse: where you fell, under the snow, dragged somewhere, or by a neighbour's fire. You wake with 1 HP, Warmth d4 and Injured. Clear all but one Chilled. Other conditions stay. Your bundles are lost in the snow, and the GM decides where anything else ended up. If STR hit 0, you wake at STR 1 and Frostbitten too.

## Warmth

Warmth is one die: d4, d6, d8, d10 or d12. Your clothes set your top die. You leave home at your top.

**Roll Warmth** every hour you mark outdoors, twice an hour at Night. On a 1 or 2, step it down one size, or on 1 to 3 while you're Wet.

**At d4**, a step down puts a Chilled in a pack slot instead, and the die stays at d4.

**Getting wet** steps Warmth down once and puts Wet in a pack slot. Shelter keeps the wind off but doesn't dry you. Only a fire does.

**Warming up:**

- A hot drink steps it up one, never above your top. Brew one at the hearth for 1 Food. It takes a slot and stays hot until Dusk.
- Shelter out of the wind means no roll that hour.
- A fire puts you back at your top. Each hour beside it clears one Chilled, and a night beside it clears them all.
- A fire away from home costs 1 Wood and needs somewhere dry.

| Clothing | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| City coat | Top die d8 |
| Scarf | Top die one step up |
| Mittens | Top die one step up |
| Felted boots | Deep snow costs no extra hour |
| Waxed cloak | You don't get Wet |

## Gathering and haul

Spend an hour at a place to gather one bundle of what's there: Wood, Stone, Fiber, Food or Salvage. A bundle takes a slot. Some things need a tool. Whatever you find out there takes a slot too, and you choose what goes home.

You eat 1 Food a day. A meal cooked at home gives you a **full belly**: your first Warmth roll the next day can't step down.

## Mushrooms

Once the back room has its beds, you can grow mushrooms.

- **Spawn** is a piece of mushroom or mycelium. It takes a slot until it's seeded, and it keeps until you have somewhere to seed it.
- **Seed** it at the hearth as the night's one thing. One bed per kind, and the cellar holds three.
- **It fruits after three nights** with the hearth lit. A night without a fire doesn't count.
- **Harvest and eat it** as a Cook. The first harvest of each kind raises one stat by d3, both max and current. Later harvests are 2 Food.

## Hearth

Come home, light the fire, then do one thing before you sleep. No fire, you sleep Chilled.

- **Cook.** Spend 1 Food, your day's Food, for a full belly.
- **Mend.** Restore a room.
- **Make.** Make a tool at the workbench.
- **Knit.** Knit a garment. A scarf can be knitted anywhere.
- **Remember.** Hold something you brought home. The GM tells you what you remember.
- **Seed.** Plant spawn in a mushroom bed.

## GM: Rules reference

**Saves.** STR is force, endurance and holding on. DEX is speed, balance, dodging and quiet. WIL is nerve, noticing and persuading. Only roll when she's trying to avoid something bad, and say what failing will cost before she rolls. Advantage when she's well prepared or equipped. Disadvantage when she's badly placed, badly equipped, or a condition says so.

**Opposed saves.** When she and someone else want the same thing, both save. The lowest roll that succeeds wins. If both fail, neither gets it.

**Luck rolls.** For anything that isn't her doing, like whether the cat is out or Nibs has honey today, give it a chance in 6 and roll d6. Equal or under, it happens. 1-in-6 is unlikely, 3-in-6 even, 5-in-6 likely.

**Picking a cost** (a failure, or the middle band):

| Cost | Size |
| --- | --- |
| Damage | d4 minor, d6 dangerous, d8 serious, d10 and up rare |
| Time | An hour on the track |
| Cold | A Warmth step |
| A thing | A random pack slot (d6) is lost, or mark 1–3 uses |
| A condition | Exhausted, Injured, Wet |
| Attention | A noise. Roll an encounter |

**Usage.** Most things have 3 uses. Weapons and the bark shield mark one on a 4–6 after a fight. A stick or anything improvised marks one every fight. Matches mark one per fire, a lantern one per Night it's lit. Anything else marks one when it's used in a way that could break or spend it. At three it's broken or spent. A Make at the workbench clears every use on one thing, for 1 of what it's made from.

**Fights, from the GM's side.**

- A fight takes minutes and marks no hour. The Warmth roll after it is its cost.
- A foe with no WIL listed has WIL 10.
- A foe that's plainly outmatched makes a WIL save when the fight starts, or flees. A group makes one when half of it is down.
- Critical damage in a stat block replaces the usual Injured-and-down.

**Stats at 0.** STR 0: she goes down and wakes at STR 1, Frostbitten. DEX 0: she can't move until a night at home brings a point back. WIL 0: she's out of the scene, as if she'd gone down.

**Who talks.** Mice, the neighbours and Murder talk. Birds, cats and spiders don't. When a creature's mood isn't obvious, roll 2d6:

| 2d6 | Mood | Ask yourself |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | Hostile | How has she angered it? |
| 3–5 | Unfriendly | How could it be appeased? |
| 6–8 | Unsure | What would win it over? |
| 9–11 | Talkative | What would it trade? |
| 12 | Helpful | How can it help? |

**Time and rest.** A task is an hour: gathering, searching, cutting through a web, going around the ants. A look around, a short talk or a fight is minutes and marks nothing. HP comes back only with sleep, with no short rests. A night without sleep is Exhausted.

**Not in this game:** magic, pips and prices, XP, levels and grit, hirelings and warbands, banks, hexes and watches, and Mausritter's own weather table.

## GM: Opening

Start at Dusk with two hours of light left, snow just starting. She has nothing to burn.

> The beech is where you left it, bigger than you remember. The door hangs off one hinge. Inside, the hearth is grey ash, and the cold has got into everything. On the wall is a calendar, the days crossed out in pencil until the middle of last month. Then nothing.

Ask her one question: *What did you bring from the city that you couldn't leave behind?* It takes a slot, and it's hers to Remember.

**The letter** is in Aunt Marit's hand, written in late summer: *Come home before the snow. There is something of your mother's I want you to have, and I'd rather give it to you here.*

**Tonight's problem.** There's deadfall among the beech roots, an hour's work for 1 Wood. The Bramble Hedge is an hour away and has more. Without a fire she sleeps Chilled.

**Running it**

- Say "roll Warmth". Never give the odds. The die on the table is the whole message.
- The hours are on the track where she can see them. Turning back is her call.
- Don't announce what a room will turn up. Let her find it.
- The Weir is far on purpose. Reaching it probably takes clothes, a tool, and a friend's tunnel. One evening may not get there, and that's fine.

## GM: Spiders

Spiders live where it's warm and still: in shelter, down deep, and in the places worth reaching. A spider's place is out of the wind, so no Warmth roll there. That's the trap.

| Spider | Stats | How it fights |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Web-weaver | 6hp, STR 8, DEX 15, d6 bite to DEX | Waits in its web, and you fight at disadvantage inside it. On critical damage it wraps you and drags you up |
| Wolf spiders, d3 | 3hp, STR 6, DEX 15, d4 bite | Hunt the open at Dusk, coming from more than one side |
| Broodmother | 12hp, STR 12, DEX 12, Armour 1, d8 bite to DEX | Won't leave the egg sacs. On critical damage, d12 to STR |

- Bites hurt DEX. At DEX 0 you can't move.
- Walking into a web is a DEX save or you're stuck. Each STR save to pull free costs an action.
- Cutting a way through takes an hour. Fire clears a web at once and wakes everything nearby.
- Out in the open on a Bitter cold or Snowstorm day, spiders are slow and you always act first. Luring one out into the cold is a plan.

| Nest | What's there | What it guards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fallen Larch hollow | 1 web-weaver | Seasoned wood. Each bundle counts as 2 |
| Gravel Pit, lower cut | Wolf spiders at Dusk | Rask's third cache, and the moles' work |
| Shed rafters | 2 web-weavers | The wool sock. The ash and the scratches are under the bench below them |
| The back room | Broodmother, in the warm earth behind the chimney | Your mother's letter |

## GM: Beetles

Ground beetles winter packed together in the leaf litter and under bark. Woken, they come as one. They're built from Flee Mortals' minion rules.

**A swarm** is d6+4 beetles. Don't track their HP. Count them.

- **Her hits:** each hit downs one beetle for every 2 damage, rounding up, so a 5 downs three. A set-up d12 can clear six.
- **Their turn:** the swarm bites as one, 1 damage for each beetle on her, at most 2. Only two can get at a mouse at once.
- **At half**, the rest scatter into the litter.
- **In the cold:** in the open on a Bitter cold or Snowstorm day, she always acts first.

Put the count on the table as dice or tokens, and take them away as they fall.

## GM: Sky and ground

Each threat has one answer. You fight spiders, hide from the crow, avoid the owl and wait out the ants.

**The crow, by day.** Morning to Dusk. It has no stat block and can't be fought. Every Warmth 1 rolled in the open moves it one rung, and in open ground (Long Field, the Brook ice, the Larch clearing) a 2 counts too. If the pack holds any Salvage or keepsake, each counting roll moves it two rungs. Getting wet steps Warmth down, but it isn't a rolled 1.

1. A caw, far off.
2. A black shape on a high branch. It turns its head when she moves.
3. It drops. DEX save, or it takes a random pack slot and is gone. Then the ladder resets.

The ladder resets under cover or at home. Show only the omens, and never name what it wants.

**The owl, at Night.** No ladder. In the open at Night, a Warmth 1 brings the owl. With a lit lantern, a 1 or 2 does. The roll still steps Warmth down as usual. The owl comes at most once an hour.

- It stoops. She makes a DEX save, pass or fail, with no middle band.
- **Pass:** talons close on snow. It's gone back into the dark, and it can come again on the next 1.
- **Fail:** she's taken. She wakes at first light somewhere high, cold and alone, and the GM decides where: a branch, a beam, a ledge. She has her pack, Warmth d4 and one Chilled. Getting down is the morning's first scene.
- **Fighting it** is a last resort: 15hp, STR 15, DEX 15, d10 bite. Its critical damage is that it takes her.

**Ant columns.** A line across the path. Standing in it strips d3 Food a round. Going around costs an hour. The column moves on at Dusk.

## GM: Marit

**The truth.** Marit kept the burrow alone after your parents went to the city. When word came that your mother had died, she stopped mending things. Late in autumn she walked to the Weir to fetch your mother's seed tin. The sisters had saved seed in it every year since they were girls, for the garden under the beech. The first snow caught her coming back. She is in the mill loft with a twisted ankle, rationing the seeds as food and ashamed of the state she left the burrow in. If she's found, she can be helped home, and a sled carries her. Nothing about how it ends is fixed.

**What the rooms turn up**
