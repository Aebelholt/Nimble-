# Cracker lines, ranks 11–39

**Status: proposed, not canon.** None of this is in the rule book. These are drafts to react to.

Ranks 1–10 are in *Sejr Arcana Assessment (long).md*. Ranks 11–20 are its next ten by verdict, revised after `cracker_lines_11-20_review.md`. Ranks 21–39 are drafted from the same template, so every card in the deck has a line.

Cracker means the end state after waking. It is what the University calls broken open.

**Template.** Each line ends the card by returning, keeping a promise, or facing what came for it. Since the review, three more rules apply: swap a cost for a new one rather than delete it; if the hero gives the object up, say what stays; and where a card fades in season 2, the Cracker carries a step up.

## Pattern

- Every line ends the card by returning, keeping a promise or facing what came for it. That is the deck's own argument about ownership, closed out.
- **Costs swapped, not deleted:** Tally-Stick, Hut, Horn, Bell, Hellhide, Linens and Unpaid Favor all trade their old cost for a new claimant or debt.
- **Objects given up, with what stays named:** Hourglass (if given to the Echo), Fairy Jar, Valkyrie's Feather, Gate Chain, Rusted Spear, Crafter's Codex, Cracked Lens (if returned).
- **Steps up where the card fades in season 2:** Horn (ignores Medium armor), Kettle Plate (die steps), Hellhide (DC 10+KEY), Drowning Scale (no Solo advantage), Sown Teeth (a lasting warrior).
- **Template watch:** "once per episode for no [resource]" now appears twice (Hourglass, Rusted Spear) and "once per arc" six times. Vary them in a polish pass.
- **Season fit:** most lines need a gig through an arch or an arc-long thread, which makes them season 2 stakes.

## Cut to ten, or build from ten

**Recommendation: build from the ten.** Use them as the template, and finish each card's line when it is drawn. Writing then grows with play and costs nothing up front. The draw table and the class guild rewards stay intact. The lines for 21–39 above are a first pass to adjust at draw time, not a commitment.

**Cost of cutting to ten.** The d20+ table, the legacy reach to 21–23 and the class arcana from guilds would all need rebuilding. Ten cards also means repeat draws at a full table.

**Recommended:** the ten to build from are the cards that already have a second stage (Fairy Jar, Seven-Leaf, Axe, Knucklebone, Codex and the others). Cards with no end state yet (Ledger, Forgotten Key, Broken Verdict, Mud, Mirror) get theirs at draw time.
