# Blind audit v4 triage

Source: `analysis/blind_audit_v4_result.md` (subagent; read only the prompt, `rules/` and `setting/`). Verdicts: 10 ship, 24 fix, 5 rethink. Claims checked against the cards and rules.

## Fixed now
- **Rune Fragment (blocker, my v3 regression):** eight runes, nine fragments; rerolling duplicates meant the ninth could never fuse. The ninth now carries no rune and wakes the stone.
- **Crafter's Codex:** Patch cannot mend what an Escalation broke, and lasts an hour outside an encounter.
- **Hexing Needles:** at 0 Thread the self-hex lands in your body and lasts until you regain Thread; it can't be unpicked to make Stitches free.
- **Tide-Coat:** you can breathe water while wearing it (its unlock and refill need hours underwater).
- **Hellhide:** the Unlock now matches the finished plate (name the demon, have it refitted, put it on).
- **Armor cards:** each states the core proficiency rule (Defend costs 1 extra action without it).
- **Loaded Die:** risk is an Insight check against your Influence check.
- **Derelict Harpoon:** Haul deals 1d6+STR piercing (thrown spear).
- **Battle Standard:** the curator holds it until a writ, matching the Muster.
- **Rusted Spear:** Solo monsters count as bosses, like Legendary ones.
- **Forgotten Key:** tier Major.
- **Guilds:** "Reader-rank" became Journeyman.

## Kept
- Class features reached early (Chaos Bloom at level 15, Creature of the Fey at Sky & Storm 3, Greedy Pact at 12): each is a limited, costed version, which is what arcana are for.
- Subjective gates, detector passives, Valkyrie at a no-death table beyond decision D, Wound-cost spread: house style or already decided.

## Your calls
- **A. Hut on Hen's Legs:** Safe Rest anywhere plus a day's travel in an hour removes attrition and distance from outer-region gigs. Proposal: Home is a Safe Rest only in the city or Briarheart Forest, a Field Rest elsewhere; the hut cannot pass through an arch.
- **B. Off-screen refills:** about 11 refills become a sentence. Proposal: an off-screen refill restores 1 pip (or clears 1 accumulation), never a full reset.
- **C. Valkyrie's Feather:** the claimed life is a cost this GM never imposes. Proposal: the valkyrie claims a service instead: carry a fallen warrior's message through an arch within an episode, or take 2 Wounds.
- **D. Sown Teeth vs Bear-Lodge Horn:** the legacy card is weaker than a regular-draw card. Proposal: swap them (Horn to legacy face 22, Teeth to the regular draw).
- **E. Rusted Spear drain:** a player can drain Rust on purpose to set up the clean blade. Proposal: keep (bosses now lose a third, not their life).
