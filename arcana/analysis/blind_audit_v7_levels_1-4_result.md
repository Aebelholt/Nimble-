# Sejr arcana audit: character levels 1–4

23 October 2026 · Uploaded snapshot `52bdf6dd0be82ca6d65194595dbbdcf3c93399ee`

Scope: all 39 active arcana in the uploaded archive, evaluated for heroes at levels 1–4 using its included audit brief. This is a deck and integration audit, not a review of every Nimble rule or every setting adventure. All five audit lenses are used. Class rewards and faction rewards are included conditionally on being earned; they are not treated as starting draws. Four records marked `cut` are excluded.

**Assessment: the deck is usable for targeted playtesting, but not ready to hand to a drop-in table without rulings.** Its principal problems are incomplete resource initialization, ambiguous failure sequences, and a few rewards whose costs become advantages. High power by itself is not a rejection criterion. The Hut, finite relics, permissive Hexing Needles, and lethal Valkyrie consequences are accepted campaign decisions.

## B. Findings

### 1. Rules accuracy

**Shared initialization — major; resolve before handing out cards.** Resource text such as `hourglass_of_borrowed_time`, “Sand (pip tracker, max 3),” gives capacity but not the amount present at unlock; numerous other pips have the same omission, and accumulation tracks generally assume a starting zero. The table rules say “An off-screen refill restores 1 pip … never the whole tracker,” but do not supply initial contents or explain every nonnumeric resource. **Fix:** Declare starting values for expenditure pools, accumulation tracks, and toggles, with card-specific exceptions such as rolled Teeth; state that the one-point rule overrides full refills only for off-screen actions.

**class_cheat — advantage procedure.** The card says “Roll your Primary Die twice and take whichever result you prefer”; Core, “Advantage & Disadvantage,” says “roll 1 additional die … remove the lowest,” with extra dice for multiple instances and cancellation before rolling. Fix is not necessarily ordinary advantage, so an advantaged or rushed multi-die attack has no defined place for this substitution. **Fix:** Specify whether Fix modifies the remaining Primary Die after advantage/disadvantage resolves or is a special extra-die procedure before removal.

**class_hunter — clearing and trigger conflict.** “A Mark clears when you confront that quarry directly,” but Cord Pull triggers “When your marked quarry moves away from you.” If meeting the quarry clears it, the control feature loses its target; “No roll required” also sits beside a resource rule requiring a risk roll on every Pull. **Fix:** Define confrontation’s completion point, and distinguish no target save from the cord’s mandatory risk roll.

**class_mage — two increments and late modification.** The resource says “On a safe roll, add 1 Overcharge” and each safe-roll power also says “+1 Overcharge”; moreover, “The spell takes effect either way” precedes choosing benefits that alter its damage, Upcast, or Chaos roll. Core spell resolution needs those modifications before the affected dice and effects resolve. **Fix:** Give one ordered sequence: roll risk, select/resolve the appropriate modification, resolve spell, then resolve consequences, explicitly adding Overcharge once.

**class_mage — failure is not a shutdown.** The trigger applies “each time you cast a tiered spell while wearing the lens”; failure leaves the lens fused until encounter end and does not clear Overcharge or switch the trigger off. At Overcharge 6 every subsequent tiered cast necessarily causes another Wound, even though a reader may take “the lens fails” to mean inert. This is a deterministic consequence of the text, not an infinite loop without player action. **Fix:** State whether failure deactivates the lens for the encounter or intentionally leaves repeated risk active.

**class_oathsworn — what remains locked?** The toggle “clears when you Release,” whereas escalation says Lay on Hands is unavailable “until the scales are cleared at the Temple.” A player can reasonably read Release as removing the fused condition, while another can read the temple visit as mandatory despite empty scales. **Fix:** State whether fusion survives Release and exactly what the temple clears.

**class_shadowmancer — casting action and level gate.** “Patron’s Margin (Free …): Spend a use of Pilfered Power to cast a spell … one tier above” can grant a free spell or merely tier permission; Core spells have individual action costs, and the Shadowmancer gains Pilfered Power only at level 2. Its “every Shadowmancer … until … level 3” description does not create that resource at level 1. **Fix:** Explicitly retain or replace the spell’s normal action cost and make the power usable only once Pilfered Power exists.

**class_songweaver — nested Call.** Protective Verse “counts as a Call,” while Call the Song is combat-only, once per round, and returns Inspiration on the first safe roll of the encounter. The wording leaves whether Verse uses those restrictions and grants that refund to inference. **Fix:** State that relationship in one sentence, including whether the Verse effect depends on a safe roll.

**class_zephyr — a fatal payment can precede the protection.** The power costs “1 Wound,” and escalation’s “it cannot be your sixth” grammatically protects the *additional* Wound; Core, “Death,” says “You die when you have taken 6 Wounds.” At five Wounds the activation cost itself reaches six before the added-Wound exception, while “at 5 Wounds you fall Dying instead” does not specify HP or whether the normal drop-to-zero Wound is suppressed. **Fix:** State an activation ceiling and resolve payment, escalation, HP, and any protected threshold in order.

**class_zephyr — recovery metadata.** “There is no tracker, only the Wounds” conflicts with these Wounds healing “only by Safe Rest,” since ordinary Wounds can also be removed by kits and other abilities. A mixed pool needs to distinguish which Wounds are eligible for which healing. **Fix:** Mark these Wounds distinctly on the existing Wound track.

**drowning_scale — empty-state resolution.** “If you try to use the pendant with no Breath” can include the intended zero-to-one Last Breath refill; a failed save then says “take 1 Wound and become Dying at once.” Core ties Dying to 0 HP until HP returns, so this special case needs HP handling, and a successful save does not say whether an unpaid Choke occurs. **Fix:** Limit escalation to named paid powers attempted without enough Breath and specify their failure, HP, and Wound results.

**oath_band — missing grade.** “The oath drops one grade” has no defined outcome below Worthy; the corresponding HP reward or loss is therefore unknown. **Fix:** Set a grade floor or define an exhausted, zero-grade oath, and state how prior penalties interact with the level-capped bonus.

**unpaid_favor — monster economy.** The example “a creature … loses its next action” is not a complete monster instruction: Core’s Dazed rule explicitly makes monsters “lose their move or 1 attack,” rather than using the hero’s three-action model. **Fix:** Use Dazed or name the particular monster activity denied.

**pitch_sap_harness — two incompatible counters.** Burden is “one per weapon stuck,” yet any creature can pry a weapon free with an action while clearing Burden also requires an hour and fresh original-tree sap. Does a freed weapon leave a slowing pip, or does combat removal bypass the refill expedition? **Fix:** Track either remaining stuck weapons or damaged sap, and say what each removal does to Burden.

**fairy_jar — unlock bypass.** “Once you know its name, you may smash the jar: it grants +1 to one stat for good” only names one unlock requirement, not the witnessed promise or full awakening. **Fix:** State whether this release reward is intentionally available before full unlock.

**rune_fragment — retroactive resolution.** “Change the result of one roll made this session, even one already applied, and play on from there” gives no handling for subsequent damage, deaths, resource expenditure, or scenes predicated on that result. **Fix:** Specify a bounded rewind or a present-tense consequence change with no reconstruction of intervening turns.

**binding_linens — stasis boundaries.** The wrapped creature “takes no Wounds from damage,” remains at 0 HP, and cannot act; this is a legitimate specific exception, but unlike Incapacitated it does not say how an enemy can end or destroy the protection. Against normal HP damage it behaves as absolute stasis. **Fix:** Say whether attacks can destroy/remove the linens and how environmental destruction or execution resolves.

**rusted_spear — depletion lifecycle.** “The next creature it hits” suggests one special hit per emptying, but there is no explicit spent/armed state; “loses 1 max Wound” also fails to specify a new death threshold, which is normally six rather than a written maximum-Wounds pool. **Fix:** Define one armed hit per depletion and express any threshold reduction explicitly, including interaction with abilities that increase the threshold.

**derelict_harpoon — miss and cut sequence.** Haul’s risk roll occurs “each time you Haul,” but its hit is what establishes the pull, while failure restrains “both of you” until the line is cut. A missed throw can therefore restrain a distant enemy and the wielder without explaining the tether; cutting also leaves the future usability of the line unclear. **Fix:** Specify whether the snarl requires a hit and what cutting does to further Hauls.

**bear_lodge_horn / sown_teeth — use the actual minion rules.** Both explicitly invoke “minions (GM guide),” whose attacks “cannot crit … and ignore dis/advantage”; those omissions from the card are supplied by the reference, not new errors. The cards still need creature size and Speed, and “armored warrior” should be identified as appearance or a particular monster Armor tier. **Fix:** Add a compact shared summoned-minion profile with those fields and an explicit action schedule.

**tally_stick — violence versus attack.** The resource switches off on “anyone attempts violence,” but the save only handles a named creature trying “to attack another named creature”; attacking a bystander or breaking furniture can end the protection without a defined save. **Fix:** State whether that is an intended escape route and whether every such ending triggers the Wound.

### 2. Design standard and resource incentives

**class_stormshifter — confirmed level-3 duplication.** Extend the Beastshift spends two pips so “casting spells does not end your Beastshift”; the level-3 spellcasting subclass’s Creature of the Fey says, “You may cast spells while Beastshifted.” This is not a rules contradiction, but it makes a principal guild reward power redundant for a supported subclass. **Fix:** Give that subclass a different use of the same expenditure, or openly designate the Mud as a martial-form reward.

**black_lotus_case — escalation rewards depletion.** First Stain gives permanent “advantage on Finesse” for lost fingertip temperature sensation, second gives “+1 Initiative” for an audible pulse, and neither requires taking the third-stage Influence penalty. These are durable mechanical gains against largely GM-adjudicated costs, making deliberate emptying attractive. **Fix:** Either treat Stain as intentional earned advancement or give its early costs reliable consequences that remain relevant after the petals are spent.

**hellhide — cap is not a use limit.** “Thirst cannot pass 3” does not prohibit Command at 3; the actual penalty is checked at dawn, so multiple encounters can be commanded at capped Thirst before a willing outsider clears it. The 30% magic-drinking outcome is also an extremely large benefit against enemy spells, with lost allied spell access as a real but build-dependent cost. **Fix:** State whether full Thirst prevents Command, triggers collection per use, or intentionally permits repeated credit until dawn.

**rusted_spear — desired depletion.** On empty, a Solo or Legendary loses “a third of its max HP at once”; the user can first spend the three Rust healing Wounds, then seek the valuable empty-blade hit. Refill by one pip, heal another Wound, and empty again gives another such setup even under the conservative one-hit reading; travel makes it costly between expeditions, but does not turn this into a punishment. **Fix:** Treat the hit as an intentional paid finisher with an explicit limit, or change depletion so it cannot be deliberately banked as the strongest attack.

**crafters_codex — retry economy.** Consult takes one hour and on failure spends Patience, but neither commits the next crafting project nor limits repeat attempts; “Things you make … sell for double” compounds half material and time costs without defining the ordinary market price or demand. At Patience 1, the book does not vanish until morning, leaving a zero-Patience retry question. **Fix:** Bind one Consult roll to one actual project and specify whether zero Patience prevents use; establish sale value and buyer limits before monetizing downtime.

**class_commander — accepted exception, not a new defect.** “Gain 1 Authority whenever an ally … deals damage … equal to or greater than your level” double-dips under the strict design guide, but the repository’s explicit audit exception accepts this encounter-bound economy. At levels 1–4 it is easy to fill, while the principal advantage aura remains free of Authority; assess whether that is the desired baseline instead of pretending the pips constrain it.

**class_shadowmancer — collection may be cosmetic.** Debt clears when the patron writes a permanent sentence, including “Their shadow answers to me first.” Without a concrete effect, immediate high-tier or extra-die power is being paid for with future GM interpretation. **Fix:** Establish what each collected sentence obliges or changes when it is written; retain the fiction rather than converting every clause into damage.

### 3. Play in this campaign

**Unlock throughput is uneven.** The Hunter requires pursuit “across at least two sessions”; the Commander needs “at least five non-party combatants”; the Bell asks the healer to spend healing an ally needs; the Wrappings can reject an unlock hit that drops the hero. These produce distinct stories, but they also ask a three-hour drop-in session to accommodate very different spotlights; absence ticks do not alone resolve a live pursuit or provide troops. **Fix:** At acquisition, attach the final unlock to an already scheduled gig and specify what absence advances without waiving the defining deed.

**City refills versus travel refills create persistent differences.** Knucklebone stories, Bell vigils, and city graves can earn one off-screen point; Mud, Horn, Harpoon, and Spear need specific expeditions. This is useful geography, but the returning player may hold a dry reward until the rest of the table chooses their destination. **Fix:** Make a refill destination an explicit available job option rather than an assumed obligation for the next party.

**provenance_ledger — whose justice is encoded?** The Ledger writes an object’s “rightful owner” as well as maker and last three owners. The table rule that readings are true, even if misinterpreted, does not answer whether rightful means original maker, lawful purchaser, dispossessed community, conqueror, or morally deserving claimant; settling that by revelation can remove the dispute the gig is about. **Fix:** Declare the ledger’s jurisdiction or perspective, preserving factual chain-of-custody truth while leaving competing claims for players to judge.

**tally_stick / class_oathsworn / class_shadowmancer / class_cheat — truthful information is an actual reward.** “Who … owes whom,” automatic lie detection, urgent wants, and a truthful bluff answer can identify a dishonest patron quickly. The table’s Readings rule supports context, not inventing a new culprit every time players ask the right question. **Fix:** Build the second decision around what to do with the truth, rather than repeatedly protecting the twist from the item.

**Valkyrie consequences — accepted, with a spotlight requirement.** The feather explicitly permits claiming “yours or your ally’s” life and the campaign accepts this lethality; the unresolved practical issue is who gets to make the argument if the rescued player is absent next week. **Fix:** Resolve the claim when the affected player can participate or establish their standing decision beforehand.

**Escalation board — storage is solved, scheduling is not.** The table rule routes new threats into the closest existing clock when six slots are full, which prevents unlimited new index cards. It does not choose among several “within a session,” “within two sessions,” and “at dawn” arrival deadlines. **Fix:** Apply a single priority order for due consequences and let merged threats visibly change the current gig rather than adding parallel errands.

**Hut — accepted scope, remaining practical boundary.** Home grants Safe Rest, Run permits rapid travel, and the brief expressly accepts both; calling those alone a balance error would ignore the brief. Define how much rest actually passes and how the hut behaves during an attack so “Safe Rest ends an adventure” cannot become an ambiguous mid-combat reset. **Fix:** Put those two procedures in campaign rules, not a new resource tax on the hut.

### 4. Power through level 4

**Drowning Scale control is stronger than its label implies, but strength is allowed.** Choke imposes Incapacitated until the end of the target’s next turn; Core makes melee hits against that target crit and ends its Concentration. A DC 13 save fails 60% of the time at modifier 0, or 36% with advantage, before additional monster-specific protections; it is not merely a skipped attack.

**Loaded Die directly strengthens the Cheat’s signature hit.** On a plain d6 Primary Die with no other modifiers, choosing the better of two reduces the miss chance from 1/6 to 1/36. Vicious Opportunist can then turn a successful melee hit against a Distracted enemy into a crit; this is ordinary synergy, not an infinite loop or a reason to ban the card.

**The Horn produces substantial first-echelon action economy.** Three surviving d10 minions average 16.2 rolled damage per shared attack cycle before defenses, since 1 misses and they do not crit. This assumes all three can reach and attack; it is not guaranteed delivered damage, and minion deaths rapidly reduce it.

**Armor is not passive damage reduction for heroes.** Kettle Plate’s Defense 18 only reduces damage when Defend is used, ordinarily once per round, and nonproficiency adds an action cost. The level-3 Commander’s Shield Expert can remove that action cost while shield-equipped, but its FAQ expressly retains once per round; do not claim plate makes the hero immune to every attack.

**Truth, naming, and routes have no meaningful “level appropriate DPR.”** The Axe, Ledger, Mirror, Key, and Hut can reshape episode selection or remove an investigative obstacle. Their reward is legitimate if the adventure remains about decisions after discovery; their rarity cannot compensate for a scenario with only one concealed answer.

### 5. Generated-content patterns

**Mechanical headers conceal incomplete procedures.** Lens “+1” appears both centrally and locally; Contract “Free” blurs modification with casting; Harness weapon count and clearing diverge. These read smoothly until executed in order, so the next editing pass should use worked turns rather than additional terminology scans.

**Subjective authenticity gates remain common.** The Die requires “genuine winnings,” the Verdict judges whether refusal is “genuine,” the Contract needs “something real,” and Mud says “You will know if it did.” Such language can establish tone, but a player cannot plan a three-hour session around a condition whose acceptance test is revealed afterward. Define observable completion with the player before the deed; do not replace roleplay with a mandatory check everywhere.

**Escalation often delegates the dramatic work.** Many objects notify an owner, faction, or pursuer. The included d6 tables improve readiness, but a name arriving later is not yet a consequential choice; give that arrival one immediate demand and a consequence for refusal when it enters play.

**Sensory passives are structurally repetitive.** Hum, glow, shimmer, rustle, heat, clicks, and darkening repeatedly function as proximity alerts. They are useful when they expose something actionable, but not every item needs a detector solely to satisfy “one passive or free power.” This is a design preference, not a rules error.

**Guild integration.** The Sisters’ Vigil ask can also refill Bell Resonance; this is efficient overlap between social service and resource upkeep, not the forbidden combat-output refill. The House supplies a gambling venue but not permission to cheat its members without consequences. The University loans an allegedly stolen Lens, so its investigators should account for the character’s recognized loan rather than automatically treating every discharge as theft. The Sworn Chapter’s authority makes the Verdict’s unjust refusal an unusually consequential unlock; that tension is productive if the player knows it before pursuing rank.

**Not in scope:** Searing Light is a level-7 Shepherd subclass feature, not a baseline level-1–4 healing conversion. Hunter’s level-5 Final Takedown, Zephyr’s level-5 Primary Die reduction, and later minion die upgrades are not credited to first-echelon combinations here. Existing level-3 features that duplicate an item are considered; distant high-level resemblance alone is not a defect.

## D. Deck level

The active deck contains **11 class rewards, 23 drawable found arcana, and 5 other faction rewards**. Its 43 JSON records include four intentionally cut entries; counting them as active would manufacture additional problems.

Resource distribution:

| Resource type | Active cards |
|---|---:|
| Pip tracker, including expenditure and accumulation pools | 27 |
| d6 versus accumulation | 5 |
| Toggle | 3 |
| Finite, never-refilling | 3 |
| Wound cost | 1 |

The names and physical forms are varied: bone, legal document, song list, armor, preserved plant, cloth, tool, dwelling. The economies are less varied: small pools plus a prescribed replenishment trip dominate. That is not automatically a weakness—small pools suit drop-in play—but it makes the difference between a city refill and an overseas refill more important than a pool’s thematic name.

The main fiction pattern is a disputed or stolen object whose former owner notices its use. This fits route politics and relic trade, but repeated retrieval agents can make distinct objects generate the same scene. The strongest deviations are the Apology Blade’s relationship, the Ledger’s contested restitution, the Hut’s needs, and the Band’s chosen obligation.

**Ten fixes to make first:**

1. **Publish resource starting values and precedence.** This affects almost every initial use and prevents the table inventing different starting budgets.
2. **Resolve the Lens as a single ordered procedure.** Fix double-counted Overcharge, when benefits apply, and whether failure remains active while fused.
3. **Resolve Wrappings payment and death timing.** Then mark its restricted-healing Wounds distinctly; do not rely on the prose safeguard.
4. **Make the Spear’s empty-blade effect an explicit one-use state.** Decide whether boss damage is intended paid power rather than dressing it as punishment.
5. **Specify the Contract’s spell action cost and enforceable collection.** Preserve the patron fiction with concrete obligations.
6. **Fix Scale empty-state and Dying handling.** Preserve the accepted ally trigger while excluding summoned creatures consistently across owners if that is the intent.
