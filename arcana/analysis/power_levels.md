# Power levels

Each card is rated against the Nimble core rules' magic-item rarity bands (`rules/nimble_core_rules_3.2.txt`, Typical Costs of Magical Items):

| Rating | Nimble band | Found around | Price |
|--------|-------------|--------------|-------|
| U | Uncommon | levels 2-6 | 50-500 gp |
| R | Rare | levels 5-12 | 500-5,000 gp |
| VR | Very Rare | levels 8-18 | 5,000+ gp |
| L | Legendary | levels 15-20 | priceless |
| C | Campaign-shaping | any | changes what episodes are about |

**Paper** rates the card as written, with the cost fully enforced. **Your table** rates it at a lenient table where Wounds rarely add up to a death. Nearly everything sits a band above the core items. That is expected: arcana are meant to pull players into play, and their cost is engagement, not gold.

Baselines named below are core rules items, ancestry features, or class features, so you can hold a card against something printed.

## Reading the spread

- **Tier labels don't match power.** Nine of the 16 Minor cards rate Rare or above on paper (Forgotten Key, Drowning Scale, Black Lotus, Oath-Band, Unpaid Favor, Valkyrie's Feather, Hexing Needles, Derelict Harpoon, Tally-Stick), and two of those rate Very Rare (Forgotten Key, Hexing Needles). If Minor and Major should mean something to players, the Forgotten Key and the Hexing Needles are the ones to re-tier.
- **Your table shifts the deck.** Death-cheating cards drop a band (Berserker, Shepherd, Valkyrie, Linens). Cards whose price is a Wound rise (Zephyr). Information and route cards rise to campaign-shaping (Forgotten Key, Axe, Mirror).
- **The combat-broken cards are the ones to lean into:** Sown Teeth, Hourglass, Zephyr, Drowning Scale, Kettle Plate. They break fights, and you have said you will supercharge fights.

## Combinations to know about

| Combo | What happens |
|-------|--------------|
| Drowning Scale's Choke + any melee hero | Incapacitated means melee hits crit, so the party gets a crit turn for 2 Breath. |
| Hourglass + Loaded Die | Two layers of reroll on the same roll. |
| Sown Teeth + Commander's Standard | Sown warriors count as allies in the aura, so their hits fill Authority. They may also count toward the Commander's unlock ("five non-party combatants"). Rule which. |
| Zephyr's Study the Form + Oathsworn's Release | Two armor-ignoring sources on one Heavy target. |
| Tally-Stick + Broken Verdict | A forced parley where the Oathsworn sees every lie. It can end an intrigue gig in one scene. |
