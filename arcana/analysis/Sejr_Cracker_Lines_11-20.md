# Cracker lines, ranks 11–20

**Status: proposed, not canon.** None of this is in the rule book. These are drafts to react to.

Ranks 1–10 are in *Sejr Arcana Assessment (long).md*. The long file didn't number past 10, so ranks 11–20 are its next ten by verdict: the remaining "strong" cards first, then the best of the "mid to strong" ones. Hexing Needles, Seven-Leaf and the Bell sit just below, at 21–23.

Cracker means the end state after waking. It is what the University calls broken open.

**Pattern.** Every line ends the card by returning, keeping a promise or facing what came for it. That is the deck's own argument about ownership, closed out. Four cards in this band already have an ending (Fairy Jar, Axe, Knucklebone, and the Hourglass Echo as a recurring beat), so their lines extend what's there.

## Cut to ten, or build from ten

**Recommendation: build from the ten.** Use them as the template, and write the second phase for any other card only when it is drawn. Writing then grows with play and costs nothing up front. The draw table and the class guild rewards stay intact.

**Cost of cutting to ten.** The d20+ table, the legacy reach to 21–23 and the class arcana from guilds would all need rebuilding. Ten cards also means repeat draws at a full table.

**One thing to settle first.** "The ten that work best with a second phase" can mean two different lists. The top ten by rank includes five cards with no end state yet (Ledger, Forgotten Key, Broken Verdict, Mud, Mirror). The ten cards that already have a second stage are a different set, and include Fairy Jar, Seven-Leaf, Axe, Knucklebone and Codex.
