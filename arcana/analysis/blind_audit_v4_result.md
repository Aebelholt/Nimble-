## B. Findings

### Lens 1: Rules accuracy

- **rune_fragment (blocker).** "Runes (pip tracker, up to 9, one per fused fragment). Each fragment carries one rune, rolled on a d8 when it fuses (roll again on a rune you already have)." The card lists eight runes. Once all eight are held, the ninth fragment rerolls forever, so "When the ninth fuses" can never happen. Fix: add a ninth rune or make the ninth fragment rune-less, so it only wakes the Escalation.
- **class_cheat.** "Risk: After the wager, the loser rolls Insight against your Influence roll." The unlock never calls for an Influence roll, so there is nothing to roll against. Fix: "make an Influence check to sell the win; the loser rolls Insight against it."
- **class_shadowmancer.** "Spend a use of Pilfered Power to cast a spell from your schools one tier above your current maximum." Shadowmancer spells cast at the hero's tier automatically ("Tier 2 Spells. You may now cast tier 2 spells; all of your spells are cast at this tier."). The class has no "higher spell" to pick, so the card must mean "cast at +1 tier". That matches Greedy Pact, a level 12 feature ("cast the spell as if it were 1 tier higher"). Fix: say "cast a tiered spell as if it were 1 tier higher."
- **class_mage.** "Chaos Bloom ... roll on the Chaos Table with advantage." This is exactly "LEVEL 15 Master of Chaos. Whenever you Invoke Chaos, roll with advantage." It is a 15th-level subclass capstone on a 1st-echelon item. It is legal, but flag it as the strongest class-feature grant in the deck after Death's Door.
- **class_berserker.** "For this encounter, your Rage does not end when you drop to 0 HP." Compare "LEVEL 18 DEEP RAGE. Dropping to 0 HP does not cause your Rage to end." The rules say Rage ends "when you gain the Dying condition". The card's override is clear and works. "Keep one of the body's hand bones" does not say whose body: the defeated enemy's, or the ancestor's, which was "never retrieved".
- **class_oathsworn.** "you perform a Sacred Decree there". Sacred Decrees are combat abilities (e.g. Blinding Aura: "1 Action (1/Safe Rest) Enemies in your aura are Blind..."), not rites. Fix: "perform a notable selfless act there", which echoes the class's own Serve Selflessly line.
- **class_zephyr.** "A Medical Kit, healing magic and other effects cannot remove these Wounds; they heal only by Safe Rest, 1 per rest." The core rules heal Wounds through magic ("Powerful Healing ... heal a negative condition as well (e.g., Blind, 1 Wound)") and Medical Kits. The card creates a second class of Wound that the sheet doesn't track. It also doesn't say whether a Safe Rest heals one Strain Wound and one ordinary Wound, or one in total. Unyielding Resolve (level 10, "Ignore the first Wound you would suffer each encounter") would make the first annotation free each encounter later on.
- **kettle_plate / hellhide / pitch_sap_harness / tide_coat.** "Plate armor, Defense 18, with no STR requirement." The rules: "Defending while wearing armor without proficiency costs 1 additional action." Only the Oathsworn is proficient with Plate. Mail is open to Commander, Oathsworn and Shepherd; Leather to Cheat, Hunter, Oathsworn, Songweaver and Stormshifter. The cards waive STR but say nothing about proficiency. Fix: one line each, "counts as armor you are proficient with" or "proficiency rules apply". This is the GM's call, but it is currently silent.
- **derelict_harpoon.** "Haul (Action, Range 12, +1 Slack): Make a ranged attack with the harpoon." No damage die is given. The Axe, Apology Blade and Rusted Spear also have no weapon profile.
- **binding_linens.** "It stays at 0 HP but is no longer Dying". The rules define "Dying. When a hero is at 0 HP". A hero at 0 HP who is not Dying is undefined: can they be healed, and do they gain a Wound when unwrapped?
- **unpaid_favor.** "a creature within 6 spaces loses its next action". Monsters don't run on hero action pools. The rules handle the same case for Dazed: "monsters lose their move OR 1 attack". Fix: borrow that wording.
- **black_lotus_case / rune_fragment / crafters_codex.** "It lasts an encounter." / "for the encounter" / "works again until the encounter ends." The rules define an encounter as beginning "when Initiative is rolled". A petal eaten to climb a wall out of combat, or a Patch on a key out of combat, has no end. Fix: add "or 10 minutes outside combat."
- **fairy_jar.** "stays silent for 2d6 days". Days are not a unit the campaign tracks (episodes, sessions, weeks). Fix: "for the rest of the episode".
- **class_hunter.** "If you roll equal to or below your current Mark count". Unlike the Setlist and Harpoon ("before adding"), the card doesn't say whether Mark a Quarry rolls before or after its own +1. "A Mark clears when you confront that quarry directly" has no test.
- **class_mage, minor.** "Invisible creatures show as a faint shimmer." The card doesn't say whether this cancels the Invisible condition's disadvantage on attacks against it.
- **Verified clean:** Grappled, Restrained, Tiny, max Wounds, Last Stand, minion rules (single die, combined attack) and Solo creatures all exist in the core rules or GM guide. The Lifebinding Spirit's Mend works only on a Dying creature, so Chain Heal can trigger as written.

### Lens 2: Design standard (checklist, refill, clearing)

- **class_commander, double-dip.** "Gain 1 Authority whenever an ally within 4 spaces of the planted standard deals damage in a single attack equal to or greater than your level". At levels 1 to 4 nearly every hit qualifies, so the resource fills from what allies already do. The Escalation needs "plant the standard a second time in one encounter ... while Authority is at 0", and a player who never pulls it never sees the GM's favourite escalation. Fix: gain Authority when an ally takes a hit or holds a position on your order, and make the Escalation fire on any plant at 0 Authority.
- **class_stormshifter, replicates the class.** "Until the end of this encounter, casting spells does not end your Beastshift." This is the Sky & Storm level 3 feature "Creature of the Fey. You may cast spells while Beastshifted." A Sky & Storm hero gets nothing from the power. Fix: give Sky & Storm a different spend, such as carrying Mire into beast form.
- **class_zephyr, memory tracking.** The separate Strain Wounds (above) are a number carried across sessions that the sheet does not show.
- **hellhide, memory tracking.** "The first time a creature sees you in an encounter, it makes a WIL save, DC 14, or has -1 to all its rolls near you for the encounter." That is a per-creature fail list plus "near you" checks on every roll. Fix: one save for each enemy group at Initiative.
- **hen_legs_hut, memory tracking.** "a food it has never eaten" needs a campaign-long list of past meals. Fix: "a food from a place it has not been fed from before", which is easier to remember.
- **rusted_spear, cost that is a reward.** "At 0 Rust the blade is clean and bright. The next creature it hits takes a wound that never closes: ... a creature bleeds out by the end of the scene. A Legendary monster does not bleed out: it loses a third of its max HP at once". Scrape drains Rust on command ("heal 1 Wound on a creature you touch, yourself included"), so a player empties it before the boss and the Escalation becomes a finisher that works on Solo monsters too. Fix: the clean blade's never-closing wound lands on the wielder or an ally first.
- **black_lotus_case, win-win.** "1: fingertips blacken, advantage on Finesse ... 2: veins darken, +1 Initiative ... After it, the bloom roots in your palm: you no longer need the case to carry the petals, and no one can take them from you." Each step is a buff with a light drawback, and the last step removes the only risk to the resource. The unlock's "the Stain advances now" is a reward too. Fix: make each stage cost something mechanical, e.g. a max Wound, and drop the final upside.
- **hexing_needles, closed loop.** "At 0 Thread you can still Stitch, but the hex also lands on your own likeness." Hexes last "until the cloth is unpicked or burned", and your own likeness is your own cloth. Unpick yourself and the Stitch was free, so the hero gets unlimited permanent no-save hexes. Fix: the self-hex lands on your body, not a cloth, and lasts until the victim's hex is unpicked.
- **fairy_jar, dominant option.** "Once you know its name, you may smash the jar: it grants +1 to one stat for good and is gone." Knowing the name is the unlock, so the best play is to smash it at once: a permanent stat bump (the class rules hand those out every few levels) for one favour owed. Fix: the stat gift needs the fairy's freely given leave, and smashing gives only the curse.
- **valkyries_feather, refill and clearing.** "Tally clears only when you carry the feather back to its stone in the grave it came from." This is good: it runs through the world and needs a gig. The problem is in lens 4.
- **pitch_sap_harness / drowning_scale / tally_stick, opt-in Escalations.** "Snare (Reaction, +1 Burden)" is voluntary, so "When a fourth weapon would stick" never has to happen. "If you try to use the pendant with no Breath" is equally voluntary. Tally-Stick: "Vynan Halfhorn sends someone for whoever broke it within a session". That threat lands on the breaker, usually an enemy, so the holder's cost is 1 Wound. These costs only look like costs.
- **Unlock structure (spec: 1-2 clear, 1 costly, 1 risk).** class_hunter ("Track a single creature ... Risk") and valkyries_feather ("Dig it from the grave / Learn the warrior's story / Risk") have no costly requirement.
- **Refill vs off-screen rule.** The table rule allows one in-city off-screen refill per episode, including "a vigil, a game of chance, a retelling to a new listener, a burial on the Cliffs of the Dead". That turns these refills into a sentence in the "last time" line: Loaded Die (gambling), Knucklebone (retelling), Bell (vigil), Binding Linens (burial), Hexing Needles (named in the rule), Rune Fragment relight (gravestone), Setlist clear (performance), Lens clear (Observatory night), Kettle Plate (Forge coal), Arch-Watch Mirror (vigil), Crafter's Codex (teaching). The GM accepted strong items, but the spec says the refill "should be an activity that could appear on the session's agenda", and these never will. Fix: the off-screen refill gives back 1 pip, never a full reset.

### Lens 3: Play in this campaign

- **class_hunter.** "Track a single creature across at least two sessions without losing the trail". With unknown attendance and one bottle-episode gig per session, a two-session pursuit means a thread card and a recurring quarry. Absent-player ticks can't cover it, because the final step is played at the table. Fix: "across two gigs, the quarry escaping the first", and let the Wardens' road patrol (its ask) count as one of them.
- **class_hunter, power vs play.** "While that quarry is marked, you know its general direction and distance within 1 mile regardless of cover or concealment." Any footprint or blood drop turns a "find who did it" gig into a walk. The Readings rule says the answer can point at the wrong person, but direction and distance are not a reading. Fix: direction only, refreshed each time you find a new trace.
- **axe_of_nine_eyes.** "Named (Passive): You always know the direction and distance of any creature you have Named." It has no range and never ends, and nine creatures can be Named. Recurring villains can never hide. "You learn its True Name" has no rules effect beyond advantage on skill checks; the term is invented. "One engraved eye is scraped blank" is unclear: all nine eyes are already closed. Fix: Named sense works only while the axe has open eyes, and give True Name one concrete use (e.g. it can't lie to you once).
- **tide_coat.** "Wear it for a full night underwater" and "Gain 1 Growth for each hour you spend underwater in a drowned place". Nothing on the card gives water-breathing; even the Drowning Scale tops out at 4 hours. As written the unlock needs another item or a spell. Fix: "While worn, you breathe water."
- **unpaid_favor.** "Name one small, specific impossibility, and the GM approves it before you take the Tithe." This hands the design to the GM mid-scene. Escalation results "5 Nemesis: someone is born who will hunt you" (decades away, so it never lands in this campaign) and "3 Salt ring: you cannot cross it until it breaks" (what ring, where?) don't create next episode's hook. Fix: replace Call In with three fixed impossibilities and rewrite results 3 and 5.
- **hen_legs_hut.** "Home (Passive): The hut is a holding wherever it stands: a Safe Rest place for you and up to five guests." With "Run (spend 1 Hunger): ... a day's overland travel in an hour", outer-region gigs lose both attrition and distance. Every fight starts with full HP, mana and class resources, and the Neglect clocks' regions are an hour apart. This is the one card that changes what episodes are about. Fix: Safe Rest only on a night the hut is fed, and Run only toward a place it has eaten from.
- **bear_lodge_horn.** "It came through an arch from a world of ice." and "Go through the arch and find the lodge it was taken from". There is no named world, arch, lodge or speaker. The unlock, the refill ("carry the horn back to the lodge hall") and the Escalation all need an invented world the GM must prep. Fix: name the world, or tie it to Drakeholm's or Terrache's extremes, as a table roll.
- **rune_fragment.** "Find a second fragment" and up to nine fused. Each fragment needs the GM to place it. It is a campaign scavenger hunt on a level 1 draw card, with no generator hook.
- **class_oathsworn / provenance_ledger / tally_stick / hourglass_of_borrowed_time / class_shadowmancer: readings.** "you see the remaining pan tilt when a creature within 6 spaces lies to you"; "The ledger writes its maker, its last three owners, and its rightful owner"; "you know who in the room owes whom"; "a witness near you has it wrong"; "Read the Contract". Two of these at one table can solve the "wrong target" and "patron is the threat" twists in the first scene. The Readings rule exists for this; Read the Notches is the worst case because it is passive and needs no roll or cost. Fix: Read the Notches costs the Truce toggle.
- **Guild cross-check.** The University "lets a Reader-rank student who recovers it keep it on loan", but the guild ranks are "Apprentice ... Journeyman ... Master". The Muster "Gives: a writ to take the Battle Standard from the Museum of Worlds Beyond's vault. Rival: the Seekers of the Sun, who hold it." That conflicts with the card: "Found stacked with other confiscated relics in the Museum of Worlds Beyond storage vault. The curator is looking for it." Is it lost or held? Fix both in a line.
- **Canon check.** All setting names on cards and guilds appear in the setting books (Roselyn Zeshe, Kaylex Thren, Sytheria, Guildred, Joslyn, Savandra, Grace Ebonlight, Atretha, Arena of Blood, Hole in the World, Suntouched Highway, Predalion's Belt, Cairn of the Last Titan, Mirewraith's Barrow and the Eternal Blades, Final Rest of the Nameless King (a festival night, so "a date" fits), Irax, City Walls, Black Goat's yellow-eyed goat sign). These go beyond the books and are new canon, which is fine as table rolls: the Black Lotus at the Oasis of Mother's Tears, the old gate chain and its wardens, Vathrex selling coal, a pawn shop in the Lower Reaches, and the hut's mistress tied to the Sisters of Dusk.

### Lens 4: Power

- **valkyries_feather.** "Spared (Reaction, 1/encounter, +1 Tally): You or an ally within 6 spaces stays at 1 HP instead of dropping to 0." The baseline "Orc Relentless (1/Safe Rest: when you would drop to 0 HP, set HP to LVL)" works once per rest, on yourself only. The cost is "A valkyrie arrives within a day to claim the spared life ... She will hear one argument for it. The GM plays her." A GM who has never killed a player will always accept the argument, so the item becomes free death prevention. It rates R on paper and VR at this table. Fix: the valkyrie never takes the life; she takes something the GM is willing to take (a Wound that can't heal for an episode, a memory, the feather).
- **class_shepherd.** "Ring Out (Free, spend 1 Resonance): ... On its next turn it acts as if it were not Dying: no 1-action limit, and no STR save to attack." The Berserker's own Enduring Rage grants only "a max of 2 actions instead of 1, and ignore the STR saves". The power has no frequency cap. It also pushes Dying allies to attack, where "taking damage causes 2 Wounds", which is a peak-value lethality pattern. Fix: add (1/round) and cap the turn at 2 actions.
- **forgotten_key.** "`forgotten_key` · found, Minor, faction reward". "Open the Way (Action, spend 1 Hum): A dormant arch opens". The brief says keys are power and a party key is a mid-campaign prize. That is campaign-shaping (C), above "Key of Doors (Very Rare)". Its gate is right; its label is wrong, and "On 3+, take 1 Wound" is a token Escalation. Fix: relabel Major; on 3+ the arch remembers the key and opens itself once, uninvited, next episode.
- **sown_teeth vs bear_lodge_horn.** Sown Teeth, legacy face 22: "Teeth (finite, 1d4+1, rolled at unlock)", one d10 minion per Action. The Horn, a plain level 1 draw: "The Horde (Action, spend 2 Call): Three spirit bears rise ... fight as minions", plus Slowed, and it refills. The legacy reward is strictly weaker than an ordinary draw. Fix: each Sow raises 1d4 warriors, or swap the Horn into the legacy band.
- **apology_blade.** "`apology_blade` · found, Major, level 1 draw". Gust (Knockback 3, save negates) and Storm (disadvantage on enemy ranged attacks) are Uncommon-to-Rare effects, comparable to "Handwraps of Force (Rare): Knockback 2 on Unarmed Strikes". The "Major" label oversells it. Also "Regain 1 when someone accepts your apology for ... one you did not mean to do" can be farmed by doing small accidental wrongs. Fix: relabel Minor, or let Storm last until you strike in anger.
- **hexing_needles.** "They have disadvantage on one kind of roll you name until the cloth is unpicked or burned." With attacks included and no save (kept by the GM), plus the 0-Thread loop, one boss can carry permanent disadvantage on attacks. Printed "Minor", but it is a legacy top-three card, so the label is inconsistent.
- **kettle_plate / hellhide.** "Plate armor, Defense 18" equals Full Plate (2,000 gp, Req. 4 STR) at level 1; "Defense 14" equals Half Plate. Against level 1 to 4 monster damage, Defend for 18 makes most hits zero. That breaks fights, which is acceptable at this table, but the two cards sit in the same 1-20 draw.
- **hellhide.** "On 1-3 the hide drinks magic: no spell can affect you, including your own and your allies' healing, while you wear it" combined with the Escalation "You cannot willingly part with the hide". A 30% unlock roll locks in spell immunity, both ways, that can't be ended. It is both an upside (immune to enemy spells) and a permanent party-healing problem, decided on a d10 at unlock.
- **drowning_scale.** "Choke (1 Action, spend 2 Breath): ... STR save, DC 13, or is Incapacitated". Incapacitated means "melee attacks that hit, crit". That is above Ball of Spiders (Frightened). With allies included in Last Breath (kept by choice) it is a strong Minor. It rates R.
- **class_hunter, class_commander, class_berserker.** All above the 1st-echelon brief: Death's Door (level 18 feature), the standard's party-wide advantage (cf. Commander level 15 "As One! All attacks made with your Coordinated Strike are made with advantage"), and the Cord's mile-range tracking. Accepted by the GM's "stronger than standard" stance; noted only where they delete play (Hunter, above).

### Lens 5: Generated-content patterns

- **Detector passive as filler.** Eight cards carry a "glows/hums/clicks when X is within 6 spaces" passive that rarely matters: "Tuning", "Warm", "Gold Barbs", "Fairy Light", "Rustle", "Dragon's Click", "Margins", "Thread Sense". This looks like filler written to meet "at least one passive". Fix: keep at most three, and make the rest do something.
- **Subjective gates with no test.** "The refusal must be genuine. The Verdict will know if you were simply afraid." / "The expectation must be genuine. The bell listens." / "The wrappings can tell the difference between courage and negligence." / "You must write something real, and you must mean it." / "You will know if it did." / "genuine winnings against an opponent who had a real chance" / "Hold yourself underwater until you genuinely risk drowning". Seven gates hand the GM a judgment call with no criterion.
- **Template closers.** "will want to know" appears in Verdict ("The temple will want to know why"), Bell ("They will want to know what happened") and Tally-Stick, and Hut has "they want to know why". "X returns only this way" closes about twelve resource lines. "Roll a d6 on the table below, or choose" appears on eight cards. "Solo creatures save with advantage" appears four times. Arrival timers ("within a session", "within 1 to 2 sessions") appear on about ten cards.
- **Escalations that hand work to the GM.** "The GM decides where it is and how long the search takes" (Knucklebone), "things in the deep start coming to see it" (Tide-Coat), "Finding one is the GM's hook" (Wrappings), "The GM introduces this threat within 1 to 2 sessions" (Cord), "Name the speaker at the table" (Horn). The cards with d6 tables (Standard, Contract, Setlist, Mud, Hourglass, Ledger, Knucklebone) are the fix that already works. Extend it to these.
- **Plausible but broken mechanics.** Rune Fragment's d8 vs 9, the Hexing Needles self-likeness, and Tide-Coat's missing breath (all above). Also Black Lotus "Regain 1 by seeding the bloom where someone you knew died": the petals are eaten, so it is unclear what gets seeded.
- **Object vs unlock contradiction.** hellhide: "Plate made over with a demon's skin" (finished) against "Hunt the demon and bring back the skin" and "Find a smith who will work it". The card was clearly written from two drafts. Fix: the unlock is to hunt a second demon so the hide accepts you, or to find the original smith.
- **Inverted naming.** hen_legs_hut "Hunger (pip tracker, max 3). Regain 1 each time you feed it". Feeding fills Hunger and running spends it. The Wolf Pelt reference uses Hunger the other way round.
- **Costs that only look like costs.** Drowning Scale, Pitch-Sap Harness and Tally-Stick Escalations (opt-in), Black Lotus Stain (buffs), Rusted Spear 0 Rust (a weapon), Valkyrie's Feather (unenforced death). The hourglass's "lose 1 Hit Die until your next Safe Rest" and kettle_plate's "1d6 fire damage and a week of repairs" risks are trivial.

## C. Exploits and combinations

1. **crafters_codex Patch plus any broken-relic Escalation (breaks the campaign).** "A broken weapon, shield, armor or arcanum object you touch works again until the encounter ends." It undoes the Knucklebone's crack (and with it Death's Door), the Bell's cracked handle ("you cannot Toll until a clergyperson ... rehandles it"), the Mud's broken jar and the Forgotten Key's splinter. Out of combat it lasts indefinitely (Lens 1). These Escalations are next episode's hooks, and Patch deletes them. Fix: Patch can't affect arcana, or it does nothing for an object broken by its own Escalation.
2. **hexing_needles self-unpick loop (breaks the campaign).** As above: unlimited permanent no-save hexes on any NPC whose hair or cloth you can get, and the off-screen rule hands you Thread. Every patron and rival becomes hexable.
3. **hen_legs_hut plus outer-region refill cards (breaks the campaign).** Run brings the Revlend Marshes (Mud refill), the Bloodrust Depths (Rusted Spear) and the Lost Cavern (Pitch-Sap) within an hour each. The table rule that "A refill that needs a place beyond the city needs a gig" stops working, and the Neglect regions stop being far away.
4. **rusted_spear drain plus a Solo boss (breaks a fight, near an episode).** Scrape twice on yourself or allies, hit the boss with a clean blade, and it "bleeds out by the end of the scene". Acceptable as fight-breaking by the brief's rule, but it answers the climax of a boss gig at no cost. Borderline.
5. **valkyries_feather plus class_shepherd Bell plus binding_linens plus class_berserker Death's Door (breaks fights).** Stacked death-denial (1/encounter Spared, Ring Out, Hold Back, Rage at 0 HP). With this GM it only removes a threat they never use. Acceptable.
6. **gate_chain plus Oathsworn "My Life, for My Friends. You can Interpose for free." (breaks a fight).** With Hold the Line, Interpose plus Defend at Reach 4 costs zero actions per round. Acceptable.
7. **class_commander standard plus bear_lodge_horn or sown_teeth minions (breaks a fight).** Combined minion attacks count as one ally attack and easily exceed your level, so Authority fills every round. Acceptable.
8. **drowning_scale plus tide_coat (doesn't work).** Hold the Breath at 1 Breath per hour and max 4 can't cover "a full night underwater". The coat stays unreachable even with the obvious partner.
9. **hourglass_of_borrowed_time plus fairy_jar / unpaid_favor / class_mage Overcharge (breaks fights).** Turn Back rerolls the fairy's natural 1, the escalation d6 or the Lens trigger. That cuts the risk out of three other cards. Acceptable, but it makes those risks soft.
10. **Guilds.**
    - The Sisters' Vigil "Asks: one vigil per arc at a sickbed or grave (also the Bell's refill)": the guild ask and the arcanum refill pay out twice for one act (double-dip).
    - The Whisper Company "Asks: one performance per arc" is also the Setlist's Crowd clear.
    - The House "Asks: a tithe of winnings" sits beside the Loaded Die refill. Its "Teeth: blacklists sharps who cheat members" gives good friction if the Cheat uses the die on a member; keep it.
    - The Grove Circle "Teeth: hunts ... anyone who brings beasts through the arches" against the bear_lodge_horn bears and hen_legs_hut walking toward arches: good friction.
    - The Hollow Circle "Teeth: hunts members whose patrons come through" against the Hollow Contract's Reaver Escalation ("sends an agent to read it to them in person"): the contract's Escalation makes the hero a guild target. Good, but say so on the guild card.
    - The Muster's ask feeds the Standard's five-combatant unlock: good.
    - None of the guild interactions breaks an episode.

## D. Deck level

- **Resource types.** Out of 39 cards: pip tracker 27, d6 vs accumulation 5 (Cord, Lens, Setlist, Feather, Harpoon), toggle 3 (Verdict, Oath-Band, Tally-Stick), finite 3 (Axe, Bracelet, Teeth), Wound-cost 1 (Wrappings), session-bound ritual 0. The spec's session-bound type is missing entirely, and two-thirds of the deck is "pip, max 3, regain 1 by doing X, returns only this way".
- **Costs.** Most risks are a DC 12 to 14 check plus "1 Wound" or "they come within a session". DCs cluster at 12, 13 and 14 with almost no variation. Wound costs appear on about 15 cards, which dilutes the Wrappings' identity as the Wound-cost item.
- **Origins and looks.** Strong spread: Norse (Feather, Runes), Greek (Sown Teeth), Egyptian (Linens), Slavic (Hut), fairy lore (Jar, Bracelet), Drendaan, Terrache, Irax, Arkus, Drakeholm. Looks repeat in armor: four armor cards (Tide-Coat, Harness, Hellhide, Kettle Plate), two of them plate in the same 1-20 draw. There are seven Majors on faces 1-20 (Hourglass, Hellhide, Kettle Plate, Apology Blade, Rune Fragment, Horn, Hut), so the Major label is doing little work.
- **Repetition.** See Lens 5: detector passives, "genuine" gates, "will want to know", "returns only this way", arrival timers.

**Ten fixes, ranked:**
1. rune_fragment: fix the eight-rune vs nine-fragment loop.
2. crafters_codex: Patch can't touch arcana; give it a duration outside combat.
3. hexing_needles: the self-hex lands on your body, not an unpickable cloth.
4. hen_legs_hut: restrict Home and Run so outer gigs keep attrition and distance.
5. rusted_spear: the clean blade's wound falls on the party first, so draining is not a finisher.
6. Off-screen refill rule: give back 1 pip, never a full reset (Loaded Die, Bell, Setlist, Lens, Kettle, Needles, Linens, Mirror, Codex, Rune relight).
7. valkyries_feather: replace the claimed life with a cost this GM will actually impose.
8. tide_coat: add water-breathing while worn.
9. hellhide: reconcile Object and Unlock; let a priest or smith remove the hide if it rolled spell-drinking.
10. sown_teeth vs bear_lodge_horn: make the legacy card beat the draw card, or swap their bands.
