## Patterns across the ten

- **Most Crackers remove a cost** (Tally-Stick, Hut, Horn's hunters, Hourglass sand). The long assessment asks the Cracker to give a goal past waking; a reward that only deletes tension leaves season 2 with less to push against. Prefer swapping a cost for a new one.
- **Three end by giving the object up** (Valkyrie, Gate Chain, Rusted Spear, plus the Fairy Jar's release path). Each needs one line saying what power stays with the hero.
- **Template:** "once per episode for no [resource]" appears twice (Hourglass, Spear). Fine twice; watch it as more lines are written.
- **None bends the power curve.** No line adds a level-scaled number, and the season 2 crossover (level 8-10) is where most of these cards fade. The Horn is the clearest case. If a Cracker is opened at the season break, it is the one moment to add a step.
- **Season fit is good.** Four need a gig through an arch (Valkyrie, Horn, Spear, Hut), and the Tally-Stick needs an arc-long thread: all are season 2 stakes, not chores.

## The open question in the draft

"The ten that work best with a second phase" can mean the top ten by rank or the ten that already have a second stage. **Recommendation: the second.** Cards that already have an end state (Fairy Jar, Seven-Leaf, Axe, Knucklebone, Codex and the others) only need their ending finished; cards without one (Ledger, Forgotten Key, Broken Verdict, Mud, Mirror) need one invented, which is exactly the "write it when drawn" work the draft already recommends. Build from the ten, as the draft says, and write the rest at draw time, before the season break.
