01 · The Drowning Scale · Unlock
  • Wear the pendant ceaselessly for a full lunar cycle, answering its whispered questions honestly each night
  • Hold yourself underwater until you genuinely risk drowning

Risk. Make a STR save, DC 14, or take 1 Wound. Each night's honest answer is a secret the GM may make true in the fiction.

01 · The Drowning Scale · Power
Breath

Gain 1 Breath each time you draw a creature's last breath (see Last Breath). All Breath is lost when you take the pendant off. A rest does not clear it.

Last Breath (Reaction, 1/round): When a creature within Reach 2 drops to 0 HP, other than one you created or summoned, you draw its last breath and gain 1 Breath. Make a WIL save, DC 12, or gain a Condition of the GM's choice from Frightened, Dazed or Slowed until the end of your next turn.

Cold Skin (Passive): While you hold any Breath, you are immune to cold damage and to the dangers of extreme cold.

Hold the Breath (Free, spend 1 Breath): You go without air for up to one hour.

Choke (1 Action, spend 2 Breath): A creature within Reach 2 gasps. It makes a STR save, DC 13, or is Incapacitated as it chokes, until the end of its next turn. Solo creatures save with advantage. The whispers tell you its name.

01 · The Drowning Scale · Price

If you try to use the pendant with no Breath, make a WIL save, DC 12, or you inhale your own breath: take 1 Wound and become Dying at once.

02 · The Hourglass of Borrowed Time · Unlock
  • Watch one full night without sleeping
  • Tell an NPC, truthfully, the moment you would undo

Risk. The sand climbs for a stranger first. They relive their last hour, and they remember you. Make a WIL save, DC 12, or the hour comes out of your own life: lose 1 Hit Die until your next Safe Rest.

02 · The Hourglass of Borrowed Time · Power
Sand

Spend 1 Sand each time you Turn Back. Regain 1 by returning to the place where you last turned time back and letting the glass run for an hour. Sand returns only this way.

Turn Back (Reaction, spend 1 Sand, after a die you can see is rolled and before its result is applied): One die, yours or anyone's, from any roll, is rerolled. The new result stands.

Sand Sense (Passive): You always know the exact time, and you can tell when a clock, a calendar, or a witness near you has it wrong.

02 · The Hourglass of Borrowed Time · Price

When the last Sand falls, the original result happened somewhere. Within a session an Echo of you arrives carrying its consequences, and it wants the glass. Roll a d6 on the table below, or choose.

What the Echo carries (d6): 1 a debt to the Black Hand you never took on; 2 a fresh Wound, which it hands to you at the next encounter; 3 a friend who remembers the original result, and is angry; 4 a key to an arch that should not exist; 5 news of a death that did not happen here; 6 an offer to trade places with you for one day.

03 · The Black Lotus Case · Unlock

  • Eat the first petal under watch

Risk. WIL DC 14 or take 2 Wounds, and the Stain advances now.

03 · The Black Lotus Case · Power
Petals

Spend 1 Petal each time you eat one. Regain 1 by seeding the bloom where someone you knew died and tending the soil for a week. Petals return only this way.

Eat a Petal (Action, spend 1 Petal): Choose one of the three effects below. It lasts an encounter.

Smoke Step (Free, 1/round, while chosen): Teleport to a spot you can see within 6 spaces.

Wallwalker (Passive, while chosen): Climb sheer surfaces and walk on water.

Drowsing Pollen (once, when chosen): One creature of level 5 or lower within 2 spaces makes a WIL save, DC 13, or falls asleep: Incapacitated for 1d4 rounds, or until it takes damage.

03 · The Black Lotus Case · Price

Each time the case empties, the Stain advances for good. 1: fingertips blacken, advantage on Finesse, and you can no longer feel heat or cold through them. 2: veins darken, +1 Initiative, your pulse is audible. 3: eyes black, you see in the dark, strangers flinch (disadvantage on Influence). Stage 3 is the last Stain. After it, the bloom roots in your palm: you no longer need the case to carry the petals, and no one can take them from you.

04 · The Oath-Band · Power
Oath

Sworn (Passive): While the oath stands, you have advantage on checks that directly advance it. The GM grades it when you swear. Worthy: a session's work, at risk to you. Mighty: an arc's work, at risk to someone or something you love. Legendary: it changes the city.

Hold Fast (Reaction, once per oath): When you fail a check or save that directly advances the oath, reroll it with advantage. The oath drops one grade.

04 · The Oath-Band · Price

Fulfilled: heal 1 Wound and gain +1, +2 or +3 max HP for good, by grade. The Band's total max HP bonus never exceeds your level. Failed: the oath clears, you lose that much max HP for good, the Golden Council writes the broken oath on the record under your name, and the Band stays shut until you do a penance.
