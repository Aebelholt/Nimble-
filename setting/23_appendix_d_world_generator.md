# Appendix D: World Generator

The tables in this appendix can be used to generate the wondrous or dangerous worlds beyond the portals of the City of Arches. Just roll for or select options from each table to inspire your creativity.

Each table produces more heroic and peaceful worlds on a roll of 1 to 10 and more sinister and dangerous worlds on a roll of 11 to 20. If you want to specifically build a world focused less on threats, roll a d10 instead of a d20. For a more threatening world, roll a d10 + 10.
