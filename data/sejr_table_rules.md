# Sejr table rules for arcana

Small house rules that sit beside the cards. The cards themselves live in `data/sejr_arcana.json`.

## Hero creation
- Roll a d20 for one found arcanum on the ordered draw (`other_arcanum`, faces 1-20). Major cards are in the draw on purpose: the unlock is the gate, and a starting hero holding a campaign-changing relic is part of the fun.
- **Legacy bonus.** Faces 21-23 hold the three strongest cards (`legacy_arcanum_top`: Hexing Needles, Sown Teeth, Axe of Nine Eyes). A player adds +1 to the draw for each of their heroes who died, retired, or was lost to an arcanum's Escalation, up to +3. In Perchance: `legacy_arcanum(bonus)`.
- Take a holding, or join your class guild (`data/sejr_guilds.json`).
- Class arcana are never drawn. A hero earns their class arcanum at Journeyman rank in their class guild. The Forgotten Key, Seven-Leaf Bracelet, Arch-Watch Mirror, Tide-Coat and Pitch-Sap Harness are faction rewards only.

## Off-screen refills
- Between episodes, each hero may make one refill off-screen, if it can be done inside the city: a vigil, a game of chance, a retelling to a new listener, a burial on the Cliffs of the Dead, hair, blood or cloth freely given by someone outside the party (Hexing Needles). Absent players may use it too, alongside their arcana-ritual tick.
- Say it in one sentence in the next episode's "last time" line.
- A refill that needs a place beyond the city needs a gig: the Revlend Marshes, Bloodrust Depths, the Lost Cavern of the Sundering Saurians, a world beyond an arch. That gig is a ready-made hero stake, and it takes the party to a region your Neglect clocks track.

## Guild masters
- Three guilds have no master yet (the Wardens' Lodge, the Sworn Chapter, the Hollow Circle). Name them at the table when a hero first walks in.

## Unknowns are rolls
- The campaign uses the City of Arches as a base and does its own thing. Nothing is canon until it hits the table.
- Cards marked `[TABLE ROLL]` in their notes lean on a fact the books don't settle (will the Observatory keep a lens overnight? does Vathrex sell coal?). Don't decide it in prep. The hero asks, and rolls Influence against the GM's sense of how likely it is. On a failure they find another way: sneak the lens in, steal the coal, pay someone who can.

## Readings
- Cards that read lies, motives, names or history give true answers. A true answer can still point at the wrong person: the liar was covering for someone, the owner sold it twice, the fear is about something else. Use it when two readers at one table would solve the gig in its first scene.

## Escalations and the thread board
- An Escalation that brings a new threat writes a thread card only if the board has a free slot. If the board is full, it ticks the closest existing clock instead, and the threat arrives through that thread.

## Long durations
- A week, a lunar cycle, a full moon or a month on a card passes off-screen between episodes. Say it in the "last time" line. Absent players count it the same way.
