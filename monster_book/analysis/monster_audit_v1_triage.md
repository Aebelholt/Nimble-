# Monster book audit round 1 triage

Source: external model audit of the book, saved as `analysis/monster_audit_v1_result.md`. Scope for this round (GM call): critique only monsters up to level 4, a party of level 3. Entries wholly above level 4 are parked (21 of 63), which removes the Hell Hound, Phase Spider, Red Hag, Slaad Tadpole, Young Blizzard Dragon, Drakkoth Faterager and Water Myrmidon findings from the active list.

## Verify first
- R1 armor guide (Word said "dice only"): true, and already fixed before the audit ran; the guide now says -10 and -20 per attack. The auditor read an older Word file.
- Bandit "fragile": true. Mortal panic protects them. The wording in the Core designer note was mine and reads backwards (Core text left as printed; the entry note is a book note, still to fix in a later pass).

## Fixed now
- **Reading guide:** new Turns rule (a monster's turn is a move plus its attacks; 1 Action is an attack, 2 Actions replaces its attacks, Free costs nothing, Reaction needs its printed trigger), a header-DC fallback for saves, and a Holds rule (escape is an action and a STR or DEX save, a crit on either creature frees).
- **Carrion Crawler:** Lash now Grapples (escape DC 12); Bite needs a Grappled target and crits if it did not try to escape on its last turn.
- **Cavern Choker:** Body shield is 1/round while it holds someone, and the hit lands on its catch, who may Defend. Throat grab states the escape DC and the drop.
- **Doppelganger Sneak:** Swap names its trigger (a melee attack beside a bystander).
- **Hedge Casters:** Writ of seizure expires with their next turn or an ally's touch; Overreach costs its next attack, not a Free action; Gravity well replaces its attacks. Writ-Binder is unarmored with HP 34.
- **Ochre Jelly (Deathreach):** each half has half its current HP and the same attacks; rejoined, they add HP up to 62.
- **Meenlock Stalker:** bright light breaks Horrid link; a hero kept in the burrow two days turns.
- **Gibbering Mouther:** the GM moves a failed hero up to 3 spaces and they keep all actions.
- **Dream Squire:** waking its dreamer (Influence DC 12) ends it; the mass sleep is now bystanders only.
- **Children of Ibraxus:** Carrier ticks once per turn however many Children; Share the fever splits damage half and half.
- **Neogi Hatchling Swarm:** Mind leash no longer needs an adult; the adult is optional flavour.
- **Dark Creeper:** it sees in the dark; the false rumour now says torches drive them off.
- **Iron Cobra:** dose outcome is the cobra's pick for one turn; the ward-word works once.
- **Hitched Scarecrow:** glare lasts through the scarecrow's next turn so the Dazed rider can trigger; fire damage is in its block.
- **Inferno Spider Moltling:** the shell burns 1d6 until the encounter ends.
- **Voidwracked Mage:** the implosion triggers at 0 HP, 2d6 within 2, pulled in.
- **Arch Guardians:** Keyed and Bound moved out of the ability list into tactics (three abilities per member again); a key shields only its bearer; Bar the way is 1/round; Flesh Guardian's fire effect costs its next Fists attack.
- **Cave Fisher Angler:** the one-line limit is in Filament.
- **Snow Golem:** Stage now reads "13+, winter city".
- **Budget corrections:** Maren HP 27 and Rapier 1d8+1; Writ-Binder unarmored HP 34; Duergar Delver and Lizardfolk Warden unarmored, attacks 1d6; Steel Hand Crossbow unarmored; Blade-Dancer 1d6; Golden Knight HP 34, Halberd 1d6+2, Formation capped at Medium.
- **Rival crews:** Contract is a contestable retreat (blocked, held or bargained with, a Hand stays); both clocks say what steps them on and back, and how they end; the Gilded Hands note says two Hands suit one hero; the Steel Hands note gives a one-hero set (Kalya, one Hand, no Asher).
- **Grimlocks:** the Waking clock ends at 4 with the floor splitting.
- **Rumours that lied:** Snakemen (coil breaks on a crit or knockback), Mimics (a crit pries one loose), Stirges (a plain stirge has 10 HP), Specter (its touch needs damage).
- **Core index:** Ecstatic outburst gets a ruling (once, at the start of the Doomsayer's turn).
