RELENTLESS

KINETIC

PERFECTION
Zephyr dedicates their life to the relentless pursuit of kinetic
prowess. They hone their bodies into blurs of motion, training
from a young age in remote wilds or mountain monasteries,
wielding speed to unleash blows that echo through their foes and slip past
attacks like a whispering wind. Speed is both their weapon and shield.

A

Through ceaseless training, they harness their inner momentum to achieve
feats of supernatural agility, executing every motion with flowing precision.

You’re a relentless force on the
battlefield. Absorb blows and
deliver devastating strikes.

Move with unparalleled speed
and determination, outpacing
your allies and foes alike.

Retaliate with precision and turn
enemy strikes into opportunities
for devastating counterattacks.

A furiously fast melee class that can deliver and avoid terrible blows.

FAQ

Does movement while Riding or forced movement count
toward my Momentum? Yes.
What does Slipstream’s move 1 space do? If the movement
takes you beyond the Reach/Range of the attack or gives you Full
Cover, the attack misses. If it gives you Cover, disadvantage applies
as normal, the Primary Die is rolled again and the lower one taken.
Can I Infuse Strength as an Opportunity Attack or Coordinated Strike? Yes.
Can I Defend or otherwise reduce the damage from Share My
Pain? Yes, but that will likewise reduce the damage you deal in return.
Could I take additional Wounds from Exploding Soul triggering while Dying? Yes, if you fail the STR save on attacks while Dying.
