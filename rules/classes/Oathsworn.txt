Through their strength, both physical and radiant, nothing can sway them
from their hunger for justice and their fervent pursuit and hatred of evil.
The mark of a true Oathsworn, however, is their willingness to lay down
their life for their friends.

corner of the realm.

FAQ

Are Judgment Dice used up on a miss? Yes, they are
expended on your next attack, hit or miss (they also expire
at the end of combat if unused).
Can I used Judgment Dice on spells? On Reach spells,
yes.
Do I gain Judgment Dice when I Interpose? Yes, Interposing makes you the new target of the attack. You gain
Judgment Dice even if a monster’s attack misses or all the
damage is mitigated.
Can Judgment Dice crit or explode? No. They are additional damage on an attack you make (but the underlying
attack can crit as normal).
Can I Interpose more than once if it’s free? No. Each
reaction is still limited to 1/round even if free.
