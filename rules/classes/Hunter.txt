THE WILD
ong before the first stones of the grand kingdoms had been laid, there
was The Wild. We Hunters called groves and fields home, just as the
elk and boar still do. We have trained our minds and hearts as much as
we have trained with bows. For there is great danger in becoming a Hunter: the
intoxicating Thrill of the Hunt that washes over your body when stalking prey.

L

Be warned—do not let it settle into your heart. For no true Hunter takes without
recognizing what they have first been given: a soul, a meal, warmth, all at a cost—a
life. And that should not be taken lightly. Wherever you roam, be it city or glade,
mountain or valley, as long as The Wild is tenderly tucked into the depths of your
heart, you will always be a Hunter.
May your arrows strike true and your bowstrings never break!

As an expert survivalist, you mark your prey,
becoming a relentless
tracker in pursuit.

Whether you’re raining
arrows from a distance or
closing in for a personal
takedown, your prey won’t
escape.

Harness the Thrill of the Hunt to
outwit your targets—set decoys,
spring traps, and strike with
lethal efficiency.

A master of the wild, adept at ranged attacks and high burst
damage up close. Class complexity:

BEASTMASTER
—— TOGETHER, UNSTOPPABLE ——

Beastmaster. Choose a Small, Medium, or Large animal as your companion. Instead of your first 2 Thrill
of the Hunt (TotH) abilities, you can select Go for the Throat! and Protect Me! to use with your companion
(your companion’s attacks count as your own for gaining TotH charges).
No Stats or Actions to Track! Your companion’s HP and movement are abstracted; it knows where it is most
needed without being told—whether defending you or attacking your Quarry. It is smart enough, and you are
caring enough, to make sure it is never put in mortal danger.
Extra Abilities? At the GM’s discretion, your companion can also do anything the base animal can do: fly, sniff
out scents, be used as a mount, etc. Even more complex actions like Grapple or Help may be doable if it makes
sense (you’ll need to spend 1 action to command it though—it won’t do it on its own).

FAQ

How many TotH charges can I hold? No limit, but they do expire at
the end of combat, so use them up!
Does a melee crit give me a charge? Yes. A crit is also a hit.
Do I get a TotH charge when anyone kills my Quarry? Yes. But
if you kill it, you may get an additional charge for hitting/critting them.
Do abilities such as Hail of Arrows and Pinning Shot also deal
damage? Yes, they deal damage as normal.
What is Half Range? Half of your weapon’s Range (e.g., a Longbow’s
normal Range is 16, Half Range would be 8).
Final Takedown: they crit you back. Can it be Defended or Interposed against? Yes. Treat it as a normal attack but change their Primary
Die to its max.
Pinning Shot, do I roll three times or get three Primary Dice?
No. Roll once with 3 times the amount of dice you’d normally roll (e.g.,
Longbow 1d8 » 3d8).
Nemesis makes everything my Quarry. Does every melee hit
now give a charge? Yes.
