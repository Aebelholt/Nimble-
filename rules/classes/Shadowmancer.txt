FAQ

Does Hungering Shadows or Swarming Shadows
combo with Shadow Rush? No. Hungering Shadows
and Swarming Shadows require you to roll the max amount;
Shadow Rush forgoes rolling dice.
Can I go over my Shadow Limit? Some spells and abilities may permit this, but while at or above your Shadow
Limit, casting Summon Shadow has no effect.
How do minions and swarms interact with Armor?
Add up all the minions’/swarms’ dice and treat them as a
single attack.
