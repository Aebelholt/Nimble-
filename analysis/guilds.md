# Class guilds

**Adopted.** The data lives in `data/sejr_guilds.json`; each class arcanum's `reward` points at its guild, and the Perchance module has `guild_data()` and `guild_option(cls)`. Names, masters, wants and splits are still new canon, open to your edits.

A guild is an alternative to a holding at hero creation, and the place a hero earns their class arcanum. Guild names, masters, wants and splits are new canon; every hall is a canon location from the gazetteer's "Locations by Class" list (`setting/3_gazetteer_of_the_city_of_arches.md`) or from the class card itself. Where a canon NPC is named as master, that role is new.

The design follows your guild notes: each guild gets designs on power, teeth, training, a store of knowledge and an internal split. To stay inside "prep is the trackers", each guild is one index card. It gets a faction clock only when a player's choice makes it matter, the same rule your overview uses for factions.

## The city around the guilds

Three forces already in your overview pull on every guild. You don't need to prep them; they are what a guild master's problems are made of.
- **The Golden Council charters guilds.** Its method is writs, licenses and audits, so a guild's privileges are a piece of paper the Council can take back.
- **The University wants them.** Its stages run from open lectures to licensing to monopoly, and "trade chapters" are already part of what it offers. Every craft guild fears becoming one of its chapters, as medieval craft guilds feared the scholastic ones.
- **The Black Hand sells counterfeit charters.** Gray guilds run the work a chartered guild cannot be seen doing. Any guild below can have a gray twin.

## Holding or guild

At hero creation, take a holding or join your class's guild.

| | Holding | Guild |
|---|---|---|
| A place to Safe Rest | yours, with a quirk | the hall, shared |
| People | a neighbor or landlord | one contact in the guild |
| Skill | none | one specialty from the guild's skill |
| Work | none | the guild is a door: its jobs come to you |
| Cost | the quirk | the guild's ask, every arc |
| Path to your class arcanum | find a faction that has it | earned inside the guild |

**Ranks.**
- **Apprentice.** You join at this rank.
- **Journeyman.** You reach it when you have met the guild's ask once and finished one gig for the guild. You then receive your class arcanum, or the lead to it (the "Gives" line on each card).
- **Master.** Reaching master takes politics, not skill: take a side in the guild's split and win the vote. A master holds the guild's seat and its problems.

**Missing the ask.** Skip it for a whole arc and you drop a rank. An Apprentice who skips it is out.

A guild gives its arcanum only to its own class. Joining another class's guild gets you the contact, the specialty and the jobs, but not the arcanum.

## The eleven guild cards

Each line: **Hall** (canon place), **Master**, **Wants** (its designs on power), **Teeth** (what it enforces), **Teaches** (the specialty skill), **Keeps** (its knowledge), **Split** (the two sides), **Asks** (dues), **Gives** (the class arcanum), **Rival**.

### Berserker: The Red Sand
- **Hall:** Arena of Blood, Lower Reaches. **Master:** Sytheria Redsong, the arena's master of ceremonies.
- **Wants:** to rule on trial by mortal combat for the Black Hand's condemned as a guild verdict, not as the Black Hand's mercy.
- **Teeth:** hunts fighters who fix bouts. **Teaches:** Might. **Keeps:** the Bout Rolls, every fighter's record and how they lost.
- **Split:** the Blood (to the death, for the crowd's money) against the Sand (first blood, for the fighters).
- **Asks:** one bout per arc; the guild takes a cut. **Gives:** the Ancestor's Knucklebone. The Rolls name the fallen Borderlands fighter, and the guild has the collector's address.
- **Rival:** the Black Hand.

### Cheat: The House
- **Hall:** Guildred's Grotto, Lower Reaches. **Master:** Gleaming Guildred, the dwarf priest of luck, coin and merriment.
- **Wants:** the Council's license for every game of chance in the city.
- **Teeth:** blacklists sharps who cheat members. **Teaches:** Finesse. **Keeps:** the Marks Book, who is rich and who is desperate.
- **Split:** the House (honest odds, steady coin) against the Sharps (the guild's best are cheats).
- **Asks:** a tithe of winnings. **Gives:** the Loaded Die. The House knows the card sharp, and which Golden Knights shelf the die left.
- **Rival:** the Black Hand, which taxes every table.

### Commander: The Muster
- **Hall:** Citadel of the Golden Knights. **Master:** Joslyn Halfcloak, the Knights' contact for hired help.
- **Wants:** a standing commission for free companies, since the Knights are about two hundred strong and already hire outsiders.
- **Teeth:** strips the commission of captains who abandon their troops. **Teaches:** Influence (leadership). **Keeps:** the Muster Roll, every sellsword company and its debts.
- **Split:** Knights-first (serve the Order) against the Free Captains (serve the highest bidder).
- **Asks:** answer one muster per arc. **Gives:** a writ to take the Battle Standard from the Museum of Worlds Beyond's vault.
- **Rival:** the Seekers of the Sun, who hold it.

### Hunter: The Wardens' Lodge
- **Hall:** the City Walls' gatehouse. **Master:** [your call], a Doorish Hills warden.
- **Wants:** bounty rights on the Suntouched Highway and Predalion's Belt.
- **Teeth:** hunts poachers and guides who sell out caravans. **Teaches:** Naturecraft (tracking). **Keeps:** the Track Book, the sign of every beast and band on the roads.
- **Split:** the Wardens (keep the roads) against the Bounty-takers (paid per head).
- **Asks:** one road patrol per arc. **Gives:** the Borderlands Tracking Cord itself; wardens took it off the merchant's body.
- **Rival:** smugglers on the Hills roads.

### Mage: the University
- This guild already exists in your overview. Hall: Kartan, Tower of the Arcane; its overseer is Savandra Thricecursed. Its split (Traditionalists, Royalists, a New School), its enforcement wing of ex-adventurers, and its secret key-making program are all there.
- **Teaches:** Arcana. **Keeps:** the Laboratories of Kartan. **Asks:** your findings, published as its own.
- **Gives:** the pawn shop's name. The University reported the Cracked Lens stolen, and lets a Reader-rank student who recovers it keep it on loan.
- **Rival:** the Archkeepers.

### Oathsworn: The Sworn Chapter
- **Hall:** the Queen's Palace chapel. **Master:** [your call].
- **Wants:** sworn knights recognized as judges in their own right.
- **Teeth:** tries oathbreakers. **Teaches:** Insight (detecting lies). **Keeps:** the Book of Oaths, every oath sworn before the Golden Throne.
- **Split:** Throne-sworn (the queen) against Law-sworn (the Council's law). Canon gives this split a live edge: the queen's sister Atretha plots to replace her.
- **Asks:** judge one dispute per arc. **Gives:** a lead to the old court ruin in Sunken Revvia where the Broken Verdict lies.
- **Rival:** the Golden Council.

### Shadowmancer: The Hollow Circle
- **Hall:** the Hole in the World. **Master:** [your call].
- **Wants:** pacts made lawful, so a pact-bound caster is not a criminal by default.
- **Teeth:** hunts members whose patrons come through. **Teaches:** Arcana (forbidden knowledge). **Keeps:** the Margin Book, every patron's known terms.
- **Split:** the Bound (keep their patrons) against the Reavers (cut free). This is the class's own subclass split.
- **Asks:** report any patron contact. **Gives:** the address of the sealed room where the Hollow Contract lies.
- **Rival:** the Children of Ibraxus, who recruit the same people.

### Shepherd: The Sisters' Vigil
- **Hall:** Temple of the Three Sisters. **Master:** Grace Ebonlight, who directs the temple.
- **Wants:** the right to tend every dying body in the city, the Lower Reaches included.
- **Teeth:** hunts body-sellers and false healers. **Teaches:** Examination (injuries). **Keeps:** the Roll of the Dead, who is buried where on the Cliffs.
- **Split:** the Temple (heal for faith) against the Market (heal for coin).
- **Asks:** one vigil per arc at a sickbed or grave. That vigil is also the Bell's refill. **Gives:** where the Bell is. The temple never consecrated it, and the Vigil lends it to the member who brings it in.
- **Rival:** the Black Hand's back-alley healers.

### Songweaver: The Whisper Company
- **Hall:** Theater of Whispers. **Master:** Kaylex Thren, the dragonborn minstrel who runs it.
- **Wants:** a place at the queen's court.
- **Teeth:** blacklists performers who sell what they hear backstage without the Company's leave. **Teaches:** Influence (performance). **Keeps:** the Prompt Books, every play and who sat in the audience.
- **Split:** the Players (perform) against the Whisperers (sell what they hear to Roselyn Zeshe).
- **Asks:** one performance per arc, and anything you overhear. **Gives:** the name of whoever stole the Setlist.
- **Rival:** the queen's spymaster.

### Stormshifter: The Grove Circle
- **Hall:** Sunset Grove. **Master:** Graysen One-Eye, the druid who oversees it.
- **Wants:** a voice for the outer lands while the city's arches leave them to rot (your Neglect clocks).
- **Teeth:** hunts poachers and anyone who brings beasts through the arches. **Teaches:** Naturecraft (weather). **Keeps:** the Weather Book.
- **Split:** the Grove (keep the city's wild corner) against the Marsh (Revlender-born, and angry at the city).
- **Asks:** tend the Grove one night per arc. **Gives:** the Mud from the Revlend Marshes; the arch-traveller left the jar with the Grove.
- **Rival:** the Golden Council, which ignores the outer lands.

### Zephyr: The Last Studio
- **Hall:** the studio in the Lower Reaches from the Wrappings card. That studio is card fiction, not setting canon.
- **Master:** none. The last teacher stopped mid-sentence, and the guild has no master.
- **Wants:** a master. **Teeth:** hunts whoever silenced the last teacher. **Teaches:** Finesse. **Keeps:** four centuries of annotations.
- **Split:** two senior students, each claiming the last teacher's line.
- **Asks:** train one novice per arc. **Gives:** the key to the locked case where the Wrappings lie.
- **Rival:** the Red Sand, which poaches students.

## What this would change

- **The faction rewards.** Each class arcanum's suggested giver in `analysis/faction_rewards.md` becomes its guild. This also settles the two cards left to you (Setlist, Wrappings). The Forgotten Key stays a faction reward outside the guilds.
- **Hero creation.** The holding step becomes holding or guild. Your hero card already rolls a holding; a guild needs no roll, because it follows from the class.
