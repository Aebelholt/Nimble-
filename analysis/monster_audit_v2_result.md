# Blind audit result: Sejr Monster Book (v2)

Scope applied: monsters up to level 4, party of level 3 (budget 9 for three heroes, 3 for one). 42 entries are critiqued, 21 are parked (listed at the end of section A). Core Bestiary numbers are not challenged (GM decision); Core presentation, wording, tables and Gig Fit are.

Method note for the power lens. Builder damage per attack is read from the Monster Builder table: L1 7; L2 10 (or 4 per attack for two); L3 14 (6 each for two); L4 17 (8 each for two). Dice averages are exact (d4 2.5, d6 3.5, d8 4.5, d10 5.5). "Ratio" is HP over builder HP at the same armor, taken from the stat sheet.

## B. Findings

### B1. Rules accuracy (verifiable)

R1. **Restrained with no exit, book-wide.** The Holds line defines only how to escape a Grapple. Several original effects Restrain with no escape DC, no duration, or no actor.
- Dig Crew, Drill-Wright: "DEX save DC 12 or Prone and Restrained under rubble." A hero in a 2-space radius is stuck for as long as the GM decides, and a solo hero has nobody to dig.
- Serpent-Charmer: "Restrained by snakes. Fire or slashing frees." Whose fire, against what, for how long.
- Cave Fisher: "Restrained on the line. Any hit or flame cuts it." Any hit on the line or on the angler, and the fall that follows ("now, and they fall") has no damage.
- Fix: one sentence each, "until the end of their next turn, or escape DC 12", matching Writ of seizure, which gets it right.

R2. **Dream Squire breaks the agency rule and its own note.** "Stand for the knight (Reaction, 1/round) ... The attacker: WIL save or Prone with sleep." Section 2 forbids sleep-as-unconscious, and the note says "The sleep ratchets: one failed save Slows, a second Dazes, never more." Drowse is also a Passive aura with no per-round cap. Fix: replace "with sleep" with Dazed, and name the aura as once per hero per round.

R3. **Hedge Casters.**
- Writ of citation: "(Free, 1/round) A target is Taunted by the Writ-Binder until their next turn ends." No save, no range, no cost. It is the cheapest control in the book.
- Familiar crow: "An enemy attacking within 6 gets a crow in the face: disadvantage." Within 6 of whom, and attacking whom? If read literally it hits every hero attack each round.
- Overreach: "(Reaction, when crit) 1d8. Backlash: it takes the damage". Whose crit triggers it is unstated, and it is a drawback ability occupying one of three slots.
- Fix: add WIL save and Range 8 to the Taunt, and write "an enemy that attacks the Hedge Witch within 6".

R4. **Cavern Choker, Body shield.** "An attack on the choker hits its catch instead, who may Defend." A held hero attacking the choker redirects the attack onto themselves. Fix: "an attack by someone else".

R5. **Subjective gates in rules text.**
- Gilded Hands, Tobin: "Pick pocket (1 Action, adjacent) Finesse contest: takes one unsecured item, never a held one." A monster has no Finesse, and "unsecured" is a GM ruling. Dark Creeper repeats "unsecured item".
- Steel Hand, Mercenary: "Offered more than Kalya pays (Influence DC 12, and the coin), it walks away." No wage is given.
- Fix: say "a pouch or one carried item the hero names", and give Kalya's pay as a number.

R6. **Arch Guardians, orphaned rules.** Sources: "Keyed and Bound are this book's." No ability is named Keyed or Bound. The mechanic that defines the entry, "ignoring any key-bearer who doesn't attack it", exists only in In play. Also "Stitched (Passive) Lightning heals it" gives no amount (Clay's "Fire damage heals it" is accepted by decision, but it has the same hole, and it is parked).

R7. **Golden Knights, Formation.** "Armor one step heavier for each adjacent Knight, up to Medium." None to Medium is one step, so "for each" never matters above one neighbor. It is also unclear whether Sergeants and Watch Guards count as Knights.

R8. **Terms not in section 3.** Hampered (Spiders: "Bite (1 Action, vs a Hampered creature)"), Smoldering (Moltling), Charmed (Iron Cobra, "Immune to Poisoned, Frightened and Charmed"), Bloodied, darkvision (Dark Creeper), Digested (defined only in the Index) and the skill vocabulary (Lore, Examination, Influence, Finesse). I could not verify any of these against the rules reference, so I flag them as unverified rather than wrong. Hampered is the one that blocks a block: Giant Spider's Bite needs it but Shoot web gives Restrained.

R9. **Ochre Jelly (Deathreach).**
- "Split (Reaction, when first Bloodied) Two jellies, each with half its current HP and the same attacks. Rejoined, they add HP up to 62." Nothing says when or how they rejoin.
- "Engulf (1 Action, Prone or Dying target) 2d6+4. Can't miss." Per section 3, damage to a Dying hero causes 2 Wounds, so each Engulf is a double Wound with no roll. Prone is easy to cause, so it applies to more than the Dying.
- The stat row omits the DC the other entries carry.

R10. **Gibbering Mouther.**
- "Speed 3 (ground within 2 turns to mush)" is a terrain rule inside the stat row, and nothing in Actions says what mush does.
- "Gibbering (Passive, Reach 4) WIL save DC 12 or the GM moves them up to 3 spaces." There is no trigger (start of turn? each round?) and no limit on where the GM may move a hero. The Gig Fit asks for "a pit or a ledge", with fall damage unstated.

R11. **Meenlock.** "Horrid link (Free, 1/round, Range 10) Links one hero until its next turn: Frightened, 1d6+2 psychic whenever it's hurt." There is no save, so Frightened lands every round on any hero in 10 spaces. "Whenever it's hurt" has an ambiguous subject. The note's "burning the marker" refers to a marker no block creates.

R12. **Ambiguous subjects.** Snow Golem "its next attack is disadvantaged" (the golem's or the wielder's), Scarecrow "Fire does it double damage" (to the scarecrow, or to the rope), Crawler "Crits if the target did not try to escape" (an automatic crit has no definition under "max Primary Die").

R13. **Reading guide against rules.**
- Turns: "1 Action is one of its attacks; 2 Actions replaces all its attacks". Gnoll Packleader (Bark orders 1 Action, then Ravage x2 "after Bark orders") and Fanatic (two 1-Action abilities, no multiattack) cannot be run by it.
- The Saves line says heroes save against the printed DC; the stat sheet counts skill and escape DCs as save DCs (Iron Cobra "14" is a Lore DC), so the sheet is noisy on DCs.
- Agency: "No converted action in this book takes a hero's whole turn" is false only through R2.
- "Slowed until their next turn ends" is used throughout, while section 3 defines Slowed as "Speed halved during your next turn". Consistent, slightly longer; flagged once.

R14. **Core presentation, wording.**
- Bandits: designer note says "bandits are fragile (a 2 or less misses them)", but "Attacks miss you on a 2 or less" raises their evasion. The rumour "They panic when bloodied. Hit one hard and the rest lose their nerve" is not in any block.
- Goblins: note says "every miss costs a hero 2 psychic", but the text is "Whenever you survive an attack", which includes hits that do not kill. The Taskmaster's "Call a Goblin Minion to the fight" has no cap and no pool.
- Spiders, Briarbanes, Lesser Fiends, Stirges and Dead are headed "Minion / Standard" with no Minion row.

### B2. Design standard (SEB, format, agency)

S1. **One clause per ability is not kept.** Word caps mostly hold, but several abilities are three clauses packed into one: Dark Creeper "Stolen blade" (damage, advantage, item swap, free move), Choker "Throat grab" (Grapple, Silence, hoist, drop on escape), Doppelganger "Knife". These are the abilities most likely to be misread.

S2. **Gig Fit cap of 30 words.** The Gilded Hands "Rival clock" line (about 110 words) and the Steel Hands one (about 100) exceed it by a factor of three. If the clock lines are an accepted extension, say so; otherwise they belong in a tracker block.

S3. **Rules living in prose.** Arch Guardians' key immunity and 12-space leash (In play), Dig Crew's "noise draws a crawler" (note), Grimlocks' Waking clock (note) and Common Feyr's "ends it at once" (ability) carry load-bearing rules. The three-ability cap pushed them out of the block, where the GM looking at the stat block will not see them.

S4. **Dangling references.** Neogi "With an adult watching" (no adult), Lesser Fiends "summon the first two" (no summon), Gnolls "same block with a priest on top" (no priest), Flying Snakes "pair it with a sahuagin raid" (none), Grimlocks "Abhuman Effigy" (not in canon list), Steel Hands "Choul gateway" (not in canon list, probably a typo for ghoul or a place not defined).

S5. **Stat-row oddities.** Ochre Jelly has no DC. Mouther has prose in the Speed field. Snow Golem's Stage reads "13+, winter city", but "winter city" is not a Stage and no table gives winter episodes.

S6. **Designer notes that mis-describe their own block.**
- Voidwracked Mage: "lower-level HP, higher-level damage". HP is 0.77 and damage is 11.5 against a builder 17, so it is lower on both.
- Serpent Cult: "Coils and fear, not poison", but the Voice's attack is "Poison. Poisoned until the target's next turn ends."
- Gilded Hands: "Two Hands suit one hero" fails the budget (see B4).
- Bandits, Goblins as in R14.

S7. **Evocative strengths worth keeping.** Common Feyr (the player names the fear), Iron Cobra (a counted dose and a word on the wall), Voidwracked Mage (Leak punishes melee), Dark Creeper's premise, Dream Squire's sleeping dreamer, Cave Fisher's "when to cut the line". Weakest: Lizardfolk Warden (Hold breath is filler), Neogi swarm (no hook without the adult).

S8. **Agency rule check.** Apart from R2, the in-scope entries respect it: Dazed, Slowed, Grappled, Restrained, Frightened, forced movement and Blind are used. Three entries lean hard on the permitted list without ever removing a turn: Hedge Casters (see C1), Meenlock (Frightened, Dazed, Slowed and drag together), Mouther and Hatchlings (GM-chosen movement).

### B3. Play in this campaign

P1. **Stage reachability.** The Stage table is d6 + renown, capped at average level, and renown is capped at average group level (the context says so twice). Renown rises one per episode while milestones come every two to three episodes, so renown sits at its cap and Stage is effectively d6 + level.
- At party level 3, Stage is 4 to 9, each with chance 1/6. Stages 1-3 (Market Square, Villas, Baths) cannot occur, and Stage 10-12 and 13+ cannot occur.
- Stage 13+ first appears at average level 7. About 30 of the 63 entries are Stage 13+ only (all of Korax, Eruwynd, Threska, Ice World, Maphotar, Terrache, Drendaan, Vynan, Drakeholm, most of Deathreach). They can only appear through the "Arch-native monsters also appear on any Arch episode to their world" clause, and the tables never say what Stage an Arch episode rolls.
- In scope this hits Dark Creeper, Meenlock, Dream Squire, Iron Cobra, Scarecrow, Snow Golem, Moltling, Voidwracked, Feyr, Flying Snakes, Mouther, Angler, Neogi and Grimlocks.
- The other reading, that the total Stage is capped at average level, would put a level 3 party on Stages 1-3 only and make most of the book unreachable. Either way, relabel arch-native entries "Arch" and have the table state how an Arch episode picks its world. Confidence: medium, since the Perchance fork's Arch toggle may already do this.

P2. **Stages 5-6 have no creatures.** At level 3, Stage 5 (Theater of Whispers) and 6 (Arch of Kings) have only human crews: Bandits, Gnolls, Doppelganger, Gilded Hands, Hedge Casters, Golden Knights, Steel Hands. Stage 4 has Dead, Specter, Cultists, Doppelganger and the crews.

P3. **Stage line against internal content.**
- Skyhunters: Stage 7-12, but the hook is the Cliffs of the Dead (Stage 4) and Encounter 3 is a Market Square clock tower (Stage 1).
- Cultists (Stage 4, 7-12): Encounter 1 is the Market Square festival.
- Serpent Cult (Stage 7-12): Encounter 3 is the Charmer in the market.
- Briarbanes (Stage 13+): Lair 2 is a Lower Reaches cellar, and "Red-weed" is a Doorish Hills farmers' word.
- Children of Ibraxus (Stage 7-10) and Steel Hands (Stage 1-8) both answer to "Faction result 'the Children of Ibraxus'" and overlap on 7-8 with no tie-break.

P4. **Episode breakers (not acceptable).**
- Golden Knights: "Surrender gets a cell, not a grave, which makes them the city's soft fail state." For one gig per session and a solo hero, a cell ends the session. Give the arrest an exit (a bail, a Zeshe favor, a short escape).
- Meenlock: a hero who is kept two days "turns", and the freeing mechanism does not exist. For a drop-in table this removes a player.
- Carrion Crawler Withdraw takes a held hero into a crack with no stated destination; Grimlock Ogre "Carry off" can take a Dying hero away.
- Dig Crew rubble and Serpent coil (R1) hold a solo hero indefinitely.

P5. **Arch Guardians against the campaign.** Keys are "not rare for patrons", and patrons pay in "key passage". A guardian ignores the key-bearer, so on most Arch episodes the party arrives with a key and the "opening fight of an Arch direction" never happens. Also "A key shields only its bearer" leaves the rest of a full table under attack. Decide whether the guardian fight is the default or the failure case.

P6. **Solo and drop-in counterplay.**
- Hedge Writ of seizure ends "until an ally touches the script": a solo hero has none.
- Specter: radiant and "a Shepherd close".
- Feyr and Squire: finding and waking the dreamer.
- Choker Body shield (R4).
- Gilded Hands, Steel Hands and Knights all assume four-person crews and a party to match; solo advice is given only for the Steel Hands ("Kalya, one Hand, no Asher"), and that pairing is itself over (B4).

P7. **Patron hooks against armatures.**
- Most hooks name sponsors who are not among the four returning patrons: a Villas merchant (Bandits), a Temple priest (Dead, Children, parked Tadpole), a Kartan mage (Fiends, Grimlocks), Erna Ironhorn (Moltling), a farmer (Gnolls). Dice roll the how, never the who, so the GM must re-skin these.
- Kobolds: the Black Hand fixer wants a crate "returned ... unopened", which is the Archkeepers' definition of success, not the Black Hand's ("sold quietly").
- Good fits: Doppelganger (Zeshe), Gilded Hands ("the party's patron hired the Hands"), Oozes (Council sluice), Archkeepers' arrival hooks (Snakemen, Cultists).

P8. **Names that collide.** The Gilded Hands, the Steel Hands, the Black Hand, Bandits' "Hand men", "The Hands" (Gilded's city name) and "Kalya's hands" all share one word in a city whose main guild is the Black Hand. "Diggers" means the Lower Reaches word for the dig crew and, in a parked entry, the duergar word for deep horrors. "Mustard" is both the Core Ooze and the Deathreach jelly (accepted, duplicate on purpose).

P9. **Lore claims beyond the canon list.**
- Black Hand pays kobolds; a Knights-Black Hand "truce kept with coin" (Knights Lair 4); "Commander Joslyn Halfcloak" (canon: Joslyn is second to Lord Bianca Swifthand); "the gold statue of Lord Salvana" (no such canon name); "the Citadel's three towers".
- "Whoever built the arches left them watched" (canon: no one controls the arches).
- Ibraxus as "the Fetid Prince" and a plague cult (canon: demon cult); Vithra, the Idol and the Night of Vithra's Kiss are claimed as canon but are not in the list; Kalya as a merchant lady and Asher as "a quiet woman".
- "Lodge children pack snow-wards" (canon for the Ice World is bear lodges and a horn only).
- Adel knows about a creeper crawlspace; Sunken Revvia and the Lost Cisterns "are snake country".
- Treat all as possibly new canon.

P10. **Neglect clocks.** Gnolls (Hills), Grimlocks (Revlend Marshes) and Serpent Cult (Deadlands) plug into the Neglect clocks cleanly. Nothing in scope pays off Borderlands, and Lower Reaches (the most crowded Stage) has no explicit payoff entry.

### B5. Generated-content patterns

G1. **Escalation ratchet repeated.** "Already X, Y instead" appears in Dream Squire (Slowed to Dazed), Scarecrow (Frightened to Dazed), Meenlock (Dazed to Slowed) and Preacher (Poisoned to Slowed), plus parked Eye and others. Four entries answer "how do we turn paralysis into something else" with the same two-step.

G2. **"Steers feet, never hands."** Mouther note: "The babble steers feet, never hands". Neogi note: "The leash steers feet, never hands." The same trick and the same sentence are used twice in scope and again in the parked Ghast and Naga.

G3. **"Low ceiling kills it."** Notes for Angler ("Under a low ceiling it is a slow crab"), Choker ("A room with no openings kills it") and the Skyhunters family say the same thing, as do the parked Grell and Flameskull.

G4. **Hook shapes.**
- "A Black Hand fixer ... lost a shipment ... N runners vanished" appears for Kobolds, Stirges, Choker, Dig Crew, Spiders and Angler.
- "Three crews/couriers/runners have gone ... " appears in Mimic, Choker, Spiders and parked Mummy.
- "A Kartan mage wants X; whoever tried has not come back" recurs for Fiends, Grimlocks and parked entries.
- "Patron hook" is a sponsor, a want and a complication every time.

G5. **Trait and encounter cadence.**
- "A hero's own X": Goblins trait 1, Mimic trait 6, Spiders traits 1-2, Neogi trait 5, Doppelganger lairs, parked Phase Spider and Hag. A dozen entries assume a hero's belonging, name or face that the GM must supply.
- The City-of-Arches names all follow "Name: [group]'s word, + a flourish".
- Each family ends with a two-option Verb and a "Monster result at Stages X" line.

G6. **False rumours about petrifying or freezing.** "Meet its eye and you're frozen till sunrise" (Scarecrow), "Its stare turns you to ash", "Its beak turns you to wood" and "freezes you solid" recur across the book as the false rumour. "Fall asleep near one and you never wake" is used verbatim for both Dream Squire and Common Feyr.

G7. **Passive used for reactions.** "Share the fever (Passive)", "Gibbering (Passive, Reach 4)", "Drowse (Passive, Reach 3)", "Swarm (Passive)". These need a trigger the format does not give.

G8. **"1/round" on single-action creatures.** Masquer Stage fright, Writ of citation and others cap an ability that the creature could use at most once per turn anyway. It is noise, and it hides real caps such as Snuff.

G9. **Same redirect.** Serpent-Dancer Sway, Slaad (parked) Chaos shift and Doppelganger Swap are one ability. Sway moves 2 and the attack misses: it negates the first melee attack each round for 1/round, about a third of party output.

G10. **Contradictory true rumours.** Skyhunters "Take off your plate and it can't hurt you through it" (inverted), Scarecrow "Six paces out it's just a voice" (wrong range), Bandits "Hit one hard and the rest lose their nerve" and Goblins "the rest lose their stomach" (no morale rule). True rumours are meant to be tactically useful and true.

## C. Cross-entry

Each interaction is marked: fight-break (acceptable) or episode-break (not acceptable).

C1. **Hedge Casters stack.** A Witch (Slowed), Masquer (Blind), Writ-Binder (Restrained, Silenced, Taunted) and Hedge-Mage (Slowed, Prone) can put on a single hero Blind, Restrained, Taunted and Slowed in one round. Disadvantage is cumulative for heroes (extra attacks are Rushed), and Taunt requires no save. The hero acts but cannot hit anything. Add a front rank of Bandits or Cultists (the note asks for one) and it is a locked fight. Fight-break, acceptable, as long as Writ of citation gets a save.

C2. **Cultists, Children, Oozes and Stenchlings.** Despair (disadvantage on all rolls, no save), Poisoned (disadvantage), Slowed and Digested all land on the same hero and add disadvantage. Stenchlings burst for 2d6 into a poisoned group. Fight-break, acceptable.

C3. **Serpent Cult with Snakemen.** Coiling command Grapples for free, the Charmer's coil Restrains with no DC, and the Voice Frightens. Two holds on one hero (Grappled and Restrained) with only one defined escape. Fight-break; episode-break if a solo hero is Restrained with no exit (R1).

C4. **Dark Creeper cancels Meenlock's counterplay.** Both are Korax arch entries. Meenlock's answer is "Bright light breaks the link" and "Keep a fire burning". Snuff "Every flame within 6 goes out" and leaves heroes Blind. Snuff also feeds Meenlock Shadow step ("never into bright light"). One ability negates another entry's fix. Fight-break; with the Meenlock's burrow rule it becomes an episode-break.

C5. **Carrion Crawler with the Dig Crew.** The crew's noise draws crawlers ("Every fight near them is loud enough to bring a crawler"). Bring it down Restrains heroes under rubble, and Crawler Withdraw takes whoever it holds. A Restrained, buried hero is the Crawler's easiest catch. Episode-break for a solo hero, fight-break for a table.

C6. **Forced movement stack.** Mouther (GM moves 3), Neogi (swarm chooses the next move), Drill-Wright's Bore (opens a wall at the end of a line), Cave Fisher Reel (3 up) and Choker (hoist 2) on a Deathreach arch. Heroes can be walked into a dug shaft. Fight-break; it becomes an episode-break if the GM uses fall damage that the entries do not specify.

C7. **Golden Knights as a clock against every crew.** Whistle brings 1d4 Watch Guards in 1d4 rounds on top of any other fight (Gilded, Steel, Bandits, Hedge). Shackle plus "cell" is the soft fail. Fight-break; episode-break for a solo hero (P4).

C8. **Arch Guardians against patron keys.** See P5. Contradiction between the campaign tables (patrons supply keys) and the Gig Fit ("The opening fight of an Arch direction"). Episode-break in design intent, not in play.

C9. **Two rival crews and the tracker.** Both crews are "At most once per episode, promoted to the tracker if met twice".
- Gilded Hands clock (6): First to the objective; Offer a split; Take a promised job; The Black Hand calls the debt; The party is offered the debt, or the names; Resolve. Step 5's "the names" is undefined. Step 4 depends on the Black Hand patron being at "Entangled" or worse. Capturing or killing Maren jumps to 6, which removes the arc after one good fight, acceptable for "rivals, not villains".
- Steel Hands clock (6): Kalya buys a building; A merchant dies; The Hands hire the party's contact; A ritual is found; The Choul gateway room is cleared; The gate opens. Step 3 needs a "party's contact" the tracker does not guarantee; step 6 is a city-scale catastrophe while the Gilded clock ends in a private resolution; "Capturing or killing Kalya, or holding the gateway room, ends the clock" is easy only if Asher (parked, L5, HP 64 Medium, 1.31) is bought or avoided, but Asher "Can't be bought".
- Both clocks use "ignores a clue" and "loses a scene" as step triggers, which are close to But+ alone in a drop-in session with absent players; the tracker rule says clocks move on "Player choices, never But+ alone". Ignoring a clue is a choice, losing a scene is an outcome.
- Both ladders use Steal and Prove; an episode with a Gilded Hands encounter and a Steel Hands encounter would run two six-step clocks plus four patron clocks and local bands. Total loaded clocks can exceed a drop-in GM's memory. A rival that appears twice goes on the tracker (acceptable), and the "Hands" naming makes them easy to confuse (P8).
- Breaks a fight at most; the Steel Hands ending ("the gate opens") could break an episode if it fires with no ladder rung played.

C10. **Gilded Hands with Hedge Casters.** Hedge Casters' Gig Fit calls them "natural opposition for the rival crew". Eight creatures for three heroes is 211% of budget (B4). Fight-break, but over the budget ceiling and 2.7 per hero.

C11. **Children of Ibraxus and Steel Hands share a trigger.** Faction result "the Children of Ibraxus" gives plague cultists at Stages 7-10 and hired knights at Stages 1-8, with Kalya "a cult sigil under her collar" in one and the plague in the other. Stage 7-8 can produce either. Not a break, but the two cannot both be the Children.

C12. **Moltling pack with a forge or Dig Crew.** Webbed Restrained heroes burning 1d6 per turn, with a Drill-Wright's rubble: two Restrained sources with different exits (cool it with water, dig out, none).

### Coverage by role (42 critiqued)

Skirmisher 8, Controller 17, Brute 5, Defender 4, Lurker 8, Artillery 0 (the only Artillery, Flameskull, is parked). Controllers are 40% of the critiqued book and most of them end in the same family of Slowed, Dazed, Frightened and forced movement. There is no ranged damage dealer at level 1-4 apart from Bandit Hunter, Mage and Ysolde, and the book has few Defenders and Brutes that are not humans or oozes.

### Repetition

The patterns in B5 are the dominant weakness: one ratchet shape (G1), one forced-movement shape (G2), one hook shape (G4), one rumour shape (G6, G10) and one terrain-kills-it note (G3). The Lower Reaches (Stage 7-8) carries the most monster results of any location, with kobolds, goblins, bandits, spiders, a choker, crawlers, dig crews, Children and a serpent cult, while Stages 5-6 have none.

### Did SEB help or hurt

It helped: the best new entries (Feyr, Iron Cobra, Voidwracked, Hedge Casters' verbs, Creeper, Squire) are one clean idea each, with a core line that names a place and a behavior. It hurt in three places:
- The three-ability cap pushed required rules out of the block into prose (Guardians' key immunity, Grimlocks' Waking clock, Dig Crew noise) and into packed abilities (Choker Throat grab, Dark Creeper Stolen blade).
- Escape DCs, durations and healing amounts were cut as "detail", leaving Restrained effects without exits.
- Reactions were labelled Passive to save a word.

The format also lost the "who is this for" detail for solo play: no entry states how it scales down to one hero except the Steel Hands.

### The ten fixes I would make first, ranked

1. **Give every Restrained or held effect an exit.** Add an escape DC and a duration to Dig Crew's rubble, Serpent-Charmer's coil, Cave Fisher's line and any other open-ended Restrained. This is the largest cause of solo-play dead ends.
2. **Remove "Prone with sleep" from Dream Squire** and align the Squire note (Dazed at most), and put a once-per-round cap on Drowse.
3. **Rebuild Meenlock's endgame.** Drop "turns after two days" and the nonexistent marker, or make it a clock the party can win, and give Horrid link a save.
4. **Rewrite Carrion Crawler Withdraw.** Say where the hero ends up, how the party recovers them, and what "Crits" means.
5. **Cap Kalya's Paid in full** (once per encounter, or move only), put a number on Mercenary, define the Choul gateway, and rate the Steel Hands as over for one hero.
6. **Fix Hedge Casters.** Save and range on Writ of citation, a defined target for the crow, a plain trigger for Overreach, damage for the Masquer and Writ-Binder or drop "glass cannons".
7. **Fix Dark Creeper Snuff.** Add a save or make it once per pack, and say what relighting costs.
8. **Fix Golden Knights.** Add an exit to the arrest, correct Formation, and replace "Commander Joslyn" and "Lord Salvana" with canon names.
9. **Fix Arch Guardians.** Write Keyed (and Bound or delete it) as stat-block text, set healing amounts, and settle whether patron keys make the guardian fight rare.
10. **Fix Stage labelling.** Relabel arch-native entries "Arch" or define the Arch episode's Stage, and correct the Stage lines that contradict their own hooks (Skyhunters, Cultists, Briarbanes, Serpent Cult).

Next in line: Ochre Jelly Split, Gilded Hands solo advice, true rumours that contradict blocks (Skyhunters, Scarecrow, Bandits), Mouther's stat-row terrain rule, and the "Hands" naming collision.

## E. Limits

- **Rules.** I could verify only against section 3. Several terms are used that section 3 does not define (Hampered, Smoldering, Charmed, Bloodied, darkvision, Digested, the Lore/Examination/Influence/Finesse skills, fall damage, how advantage and disadvantage stack). I treated them as unverified. Confidence in rules findings R1-R7 and R9-R14 is high; R8 is a list of unverified terms, not errors.
- **Power.** I estimated hero damage from the builder table, not hero sheets, so I did not know a L3 hero's real per-attack output or HP, and "Medium armor blunts normal hits" is inferred. Group math used the stated budget (9 for three L3 heroes). Confidence: high for the arithmetic, medium for the claim that Medium bodies stall fights. Core numbers were taken as given.
- **Stage reachability.** The reading "Stage = d6 + level" comes from two statements in the context and the 13+ row. The Perchance fork's Arch toggle may route Arch episodes differently. Confidence: medium.
- **Canon.** I could check only against the section 4 list; any name I call new may exist in the setting books. Vithra, Fetid Prince, Salvana, Commander Joslyn, Choul, Verosa and the Abhuman Effigy are the ones I could not place.
- **Play feel.** No table testing. Solo-play claims are reasoned from the text, not run. Confidence: medium.
- **Scope.** Parked entries were not critiqued; where an in-scope family shares text with a parked member (Steel Hands' Asher, Grimlocks' Chanter, Snakemen's Giant Cobra) I used the shared text only.
- **Overall confidence.** Section A verdicts: medium-high. Section B1: high. B2: medium-high. B3: medium. B4: medium-high. B5: high (observable repetition). Sections C and D: medium.
