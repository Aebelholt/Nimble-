## The Ancestor's Knucklebone

**Object.** A knucklebone worn smooth by a hundred years of handling, threaded on gut string. Taken from the body of a Berserker who died fighting in the Borderlands and was never retrieved. **Unlock.**
- Defeat an enemy in single combat without any ally's assistance.
- Keep one of the body's hand bones.
- Spend a night alone with it before you sleep, and see how the ancestor fought.
- Risk: Whatever hunted the ancestor still wants the bone, and the night alone is when it finds you.

**Resource: Fury Echo (pip tracker).** Fury Echo (pip tracker, max 3). **Escalation.** If you drop to 0 HP with Death's Door active, the knucklebone cracks and stops working. Replace it with another ancestor's relic from the Borderlands, or take one from a worthy enemy. The GM decides where it is and how long the search takes. Whatever hunted the ancestor learns the bone has moved.

*Changelog:* Reworked. Echo now fills by retelling, not by Fury Die matches (double-dipping); Death's Door paid up front (peak-value lethality); risk added; em dash removed; retelling venue: Sevenge and Gam's Public House (3_gazetteer).

## The Loaded Die

**Unlock.**

- Risk: The Black Hand card sharp hears whose hands the die is in.

**Resource: Fixes (pip tracker).** Fixes (pip tracker, max 4). Fixes do not refill automatically. **Escalation.** When all 4 Fixes are spent, the die gets you anyway. `class_hunter` · 1st echelon · Hunter

**Object.** A length of braided sinew hung with small carved tokens: a boot print, a feather, a fang, and three others worn unrecognisable by handling. **Unlock.**

*Changelog:* Marks now one count with one clearing rule (confront or Cut); Safe Rest wipe removed; risk line added; em dashes removed; Slowed uses the rules wording.
