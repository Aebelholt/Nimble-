# Audit round 5 triage

Two audits of the v4 prompt, both run before the v4 fixes landed:
- **Session audit** (`analysis/blind_audit_v5_session_result.md`, not blind: the same session wrote audit v3). 19 ship, 18 fix, 2 rethink.

Already fixed in v4 before these arrived: armor proficiency, Harpoon damage, Codex Patch scope, Rusted Spear Solo bosses, Hexing self-hex loop, Tide-Coat breathing, Hellhide unlock.

## Fixed now
- **Prompt header** said 35 cards; it now counts the cards.
- **Gate Chain:** Hold the Line was a Reaction, which costs the action it saves. Now Free when you Interpose.
- **Rusted Spear:** Spare triggers when a creature would die, so a Legendary monster's Last Stand still runs.
- **Hollow Contract:** Debt above 4 is lost; the patron collects at 4 or more.
- **Cracked Lens:** the spell takes effect either way and the lens fails after it; one safe-roll power per cast, by subclass.
- **Tracking Cord:** roll before the new Mark is added.
- **Hourglass:** one die, yours or anyone's, from any roll.
- **Fairy Jar:** the fey beyond the Arch of Flowers know its name.
- **Apology Blade:** Gust's Line runs from you in a direction you choose and hits allies too.

## Disagreed
- Advantage stacking (Commander): Nimble stacks advantage by rule.
- Minion procedure (Sown Teeth, Horn): the GM guide's minion rules cover it; the external auditor lacked the GM guide.
- Hexing Needles scope, Oathsworn Lay on Hands lock, Rusted Spear's lost max Wound: decided earlier.
- Ring Out "contradictory state": the card states it plainly (still Dying, no action limit, no attack save).
- "Martial" weapon group: the session auditor withdrew it.
