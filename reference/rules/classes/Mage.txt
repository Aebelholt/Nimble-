omprehending the Tapestry of Mana is no trivial endeavor—it will
yield its arcane secrets only to the earnest seeker of knowledge. A
mage refines their natural gifts through scholarly pursuit, with
tomes and parchments as their companions, alongside the sage counsel of
a seasoned mentor or three!

C

Yet, once the adept learns to discern the subtleties of its intricate patterns
as it unfurls and meanders into the ethereal expanse—it is in that moment
the apprentice truly ascends to the mantle of mage. This is the chosen path
of those who would wield authority over the elements. Elemental power
flows through all things… find it, study it, and harness it!

Customize your spells: stretch
time, gain extra-dimensional
vision, or echo cast multiple
spells!

Rain fire from the heavens,
freeze enemies in their tracks,
and strike with thunderous
bolts of lightning.

Demand Control or lean into the
Chaos! What awaits you? Diamond Skin? Elemental Entanglement? Liquefied Legs?!

Force of Chaos. Whenever you cast a spell, you can choose to spend 1 less
mana by Invoking Chaos: Roll on the Chaos Table. Whenever you crit you may
also Invoke Chaos.
Tempest Mage. Learn 1 cantrip and 1 tiered spell from the Wind school.
Chaos Lash. (1/encounter) Reaction (when an enemy moves adjacent to you):
They are pushed back 2 spaces, and on a failed WIL save, knocked Prone as well.
Invoke Chaos.

LEVEL 11 Thrive in Chaos. Whenever you Invoke Chaos, you may roll twice and cause

both effects. (1/Safe Rest) You may choose which roll to use instead.

Can I cast more than 1 defensive spell at a time? No.
Invoker of Surges: Does taking damage from Power Surge
Charge me? Yes, any lightning damage gives you the Charged condition.
How does Lose Control work? You’ll likely never want to pick that
option but the 4th time in an encounter you Demand Control, you
must pick it. The GM uses I INSIST, ELEMENTAL AFFLICTION, and NO;
choosing the targets, spells, and other effects.

LEVEL 15 Master of Chaos. Whenever you Invoke Chaos, roll with advantage.

Where’s the Chaos Table? It’s a secret for the GM only! Suffice it to say, rolling a 1 is really bad, but
rolling a 20 is AWESOME—but if you’re sure you want to spoil it, you can find it on the back inside cover
of the GM’s Guide. Let chaos reign!
