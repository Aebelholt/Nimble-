COMMANDER

SOLDIER. WARRIOR.

FEARLESS

LEADER
An “empire” isn’t an EMPIRE until they have their own academy of command: Famed
schools where these supremely capable soldiers are trained in battlefield tactics
and leadership.
Among the most skilled soldiers in the world, Commanders are masters of every
weapon and form of close combat. A small cohort of soldiers led by a trained Commander is more feared than innumerable legions without one.

Issue powerful tactical orders to
your allies, bolster their abilities, and coordinate devastating strikes.

Exert mastery over all weapon
types, wielding them with
deadly efficiency—ensuring
victory against any foe.

Lead your party to triumph
through cunning strategy,
tactical prowess, and decisive action.

Highly tactical, teamwork-focused melee class. A party’s force multiplier.

—— STEEL MEETS SPELL ——

Arcane Command. Your focus on the arcane causes you to lose access to Weapon Mastery and
Combat Tactics, but you now gain INT mana when you roll Initiative (this mana is lost if unspent when
combat ends). Whenever you could choose a Combat Tactic or Weapon Mastery, instead choose another
Commander’s Order or a tier 1 (or lower) spell from any spell school. Your Commander’s Orders are
also empowered with magical power:
• Face Me! (Glimmering Decree). Free Reaction (whenever an enemy crits or attacks a Dying ally):
That enemy takes STR d8 radiant damage (ignoring armor), is pulled up to 4 spaces toward you,
and is Taunted by you until you drop to 0 HP.
• Move it! Move it! (Borne upon the Wind). When you roll Initiative, you may give yourself
and an ally advantage on the roll, +3 Speed, and the ability to fly for 1 round. Then, you both can
also move for free.

• Hold the Line! (Crystalline Armor). (1/encounter) Reaction (when an ally drops to 0 HP):
Command them to continue the fight! Set their HP to 3× your LVL. Additionally, they gain that
many temp HP. Enemies who reduce this temp HP in melee have their Speed halved until the
end of their next turn.
• Reposition! (Flashstep). Action: Command 1 ally to move up to their Speed (or 2 allies up to
half their Speed) for free; you may exchange places with one of them. You may use this as a reaction
on an ally’s turn.

• I Can Do This ALL DAY! (Rising Phoenix). (1/encounter) Reaction (when you would drop to
0 HP): You may expend any number of Hit Dice, set your HP to the sum rolled instead, and deal
that much fire damage to each enemy within Reach 2 of you. They gain the Smoldering condition.
• Commanding Presence (Withering Presence). Creatures you Command suffer necrotic
damage equal to the max value of your Combat Die (ignoring armor) and are considered undead
for 1 round.

Spellforge. When you roll Initiative you may cast Enchant Weapon for free (can be upcast
as normal by spending additional mana).

How do AoE melee attacks work? The selected area
must make sense: if an ally is between two enemies you
can’t strike both enemies and avoid the ally!
