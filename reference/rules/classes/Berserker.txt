WRATH

& RUIN
The Berserker is destruction. He knows not fatigue nor caution—both driven
away in a relentless fury. Even death fears to take a Berserker before his
battle Rage is satiated. Once a Berserker has begun to fight, he only grows
stronger, fueled by battle-lust and unending Rage.
The deadliest among them is not the well-rested, but those who are
pushed to the brink through combat. It matters not what a Berserker
wields, be it axe or sword; flesh will be hewn from bone and heads from
shoulders. Many have caved under the Berserker’s primal might—sword
and spell are but straw in the wake of unbridled RAGE.

Become a raging, damage-dealing machine. Greet
death as an old friend and
keep on fighting!

Deal out incredible amounts
of brutality: the longer a fight
goes on, the more your Rage
intensifies.

Use your Savage Arsenal.
Choose abilities to crush
your foes and laugh in the
face of death!

FAQ

Fury Dice. These are NOT expended when making an attack. You keep
accumulating them as your fury grows, they are only lost when your
Rage ends (at the end of combat or when you gain the Dying condition)
or when you expend them to use an ability like “That all you got?!”
Intensifying Fury. You can still Rage 1/turn with this feature. That
is: you can still spend an action to Rage and roll an additional Fury Die.
Enduring Rage. You cannot Rage again on a turn you’ve triggered this
ability since Rage is limited to 1/turn. “Free” means only that you don’t
have to use an action or other resources to do it.
This ability does stack with Intensifying Fury since both happen “at the
beginning of your turn” (example: when you first gain the Dying condition your Rage ends and you lose all your Fury Dice; then at the beginning of your turn, you Rage for free from Enduring Rage; then roll an
additional Fury Die for free from Intensifying Fury).
Path of the Mountainheart: Unbreakable. This applies to ANY condition listed in the Core Rules or any other special condition applied by
monsters or environmental effects. If you negate the Dying condition
you drop to 1 HP instead.
