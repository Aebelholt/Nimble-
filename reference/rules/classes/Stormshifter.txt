MASTER OF

FANG & STORM
he trees whisper their excitement, the clouds sing with glee.
Rejoice! The Master of Storm & Fang arrives. Legend speaks
of an ancient union between mortal and dryad—beings capable
of wielding lightning and tempests as others wield blade and shield. This
is but half the tale.

T

Wanderers recount encounters with beings, some wise and benevolent,
others fearsome, with claws that sparked with lightning and pelts sharp as
daggers. They are as one with storm and beast, embodying the serene beauty
and raw power of nature’s grace.

Aid your allies in whatever
role is most needed. Damage,
healing, or control.

Wade into the fray as a shapeshifted
beast ranging from friendly to vicious,
indomitable, or HORRIBLE.

Cast powerful spells from a
distance to strike down, control, mend, and more.

A high utility class that can both harm and heal with equal mastery!

DIREBEAST FORMS
Fearsome Beast

1 Action

Transform into a Large beast. Gain WIL Defense, WIL+LVL temp HP, advantage
on STR saves, and:

Fearsome. Whenever you Interpose or Defend, you may spend 1 mana
to force the attacker to reroll the attack (you must choose either result).
Gore. 1 Action: 1d8+LVL damage, on hit: gain LVL temp HP.

Beast of the Pack

1 Action

Transform into a Medium beast. Gain +DEX Speed, you can move DEX
spaces for free each turn, advantage on DEX saves, and:
Lightning Step. (1/turn) Spend 1 mana to teleport DEX spaces for free.

Frenzy. 1 Action: 1d6+LVL damage. Your next attack gains increasing
advantage instead of disadvantage. This bonus resets whenever you crit,
or after you use a different attack.

Beast of Nightmares

1 Action

Transform into any Tiny beast or insect (provided it is horrible). Gain:

Silent But Deadly. Speed: 2. Attackers cannot target you until you become
conspicuous (e.g., attacking or being seen transforming). Attacks against
you have disadvantage. You cannot Defend or Interpose and any damage
ends this form.
Sting. 1 Action: (1/round, Reach 0) 1d4+3×LVL acid damage (ignoring
Armor), on crit: Dazed.

Does shifting into a harmless beast have a time limit or take a charge? No.
Can I cast spells while shifted? Yes. But it ends the form (unless you have a special ability
that allows you to maintain it).
When shifting between one Direbeast Form and another, do I keep the temp HP and
Frenzy bonuses? Yes, though they end when combat does if unspent. The other passive effects
(e.g., extra Defense or movement) are not maintained.
Can I build up advantage with Frenzy then cast a spell with it? Yes. Have fun!
Does the temp HP from Fearsome Beast and Gore stack? No. Temp HP do not stack.
Can I Beastshift while Dying? Yes. Although dropping to 0 HP ends any currently active
forms, you can shift again while Dying.
